# Agent 10 — Cognitive Load Analyst — Phase 1.1 Clutter Reduction Recommendations

**Agent:** 10 — Cognitive Load Analyst  
**Date:** 2026-05-18  
**Reference Screenshot Analyzed:** Current dense task execution view  
**Target Reduction:** 70%+ by reducing mental effort on first load

---

## 1. Current Clutter Diagnosis

The current view requires users to constantly filter and prioritize in their head because too much is visible. This creates high extraneous cognitive load.

---

## 2. 70% Reduction Strategy

### 2.1 Move to Right-Side Veil Drawer
By moving the majority of content into the Veil, the default view only presents the information needed for triage and quick decisions. This dramatically lowers cognitive load.

### 2.2 Move to Hover Cards / Previews
Quick answers to common questions without forcing the user to hold multiple pieces of information in working memory.

### 2.3 Move to Modals
Focused work where the user can give full attention without distraction.

### 2.4 Remove or Collapse Entirely
Anything that forces the user to "read around" noise or ignore irrelevant sections.

### 2.5 React Component Opportunities
- Components that support "progressive revelation" (only show more when asked)
- Clear visual hierarchy that guides attention automatically

---

## 3. Impact on Default View

The new minimal list + Veil model is designed around how humans actually process information: one primary task at a time, with easy access to depth when needed.

This is one of the biggest contributors to the "70% less cluttered" feeling.

---

## 4. Glassmorphism Application (Veil Glass Rules)

The Veil should feel like a calm, contained space where the user can focus without the pressure of the full list behind it.

---

## 5. Risks & Trade-offs

- Risk: Some users may initially feel they have "less information" (mitigate with excellent hover previews and fast Veil)
- Benefit: Much lower mental fatigue, especially for users who manage dozens of tasks per day

---

## 6. Dependencies on Other Agents

- Agent 06: Minimal list must be scannable with low effort
- Agent 03: Veil must feel like a relief, not another place to work hard
- Agent 15: Scanning paths must be optimized for low cognitive load

---

## 7. Measurement & Validation Approach

- NASA-TLX style quick cognitive load questions
- Task completion rate and error rate in simulated high-volume scenarios

---
