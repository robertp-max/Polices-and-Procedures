The Artifact Lifecycle

Care Indeed does not treat policies as static documents. They are managed as living governance artifacts progressing through a strict lifecycle. This ensures that no standard is implemented without proper vetting, and no policy remains active once it becomes obsolete.

Automated Review Cycles

To maintain CMS and OIG compliance, active policies cannot remain stagnant. The framework automatically triggers a transition back to "Under Review" based on defined cycles:

  • Annual Cycle (86.9% of Policies) Mandatory review every 12 months. Applied to all high-risk, regulatory, and clinical operations policies.
  • Biennial Cycle (10.7% of Policies) Mandatory review every 24 months. Applied to lower-risk operational and administrative standards.
  • Triggered Reviews (2.5% of Policies) Immediate review initiated out-of-cycle due to regulatory changes, adverse events, or audit findings.

Version Control & Traceability

Every iteration of a policy generates a new Version Number. Deprecated or superseded artifacts are never deleted; they are archived as "SUPERSEDED — NOT FOR USE" to provide an absolute audit trail for historical litigation and CMS survey look-back periods.
