# 2022 Handbook to 2026 Draft — Replacement Map

The 2022 PDF is retained as historical evidence only. It should not be issued for new acknowledgment. This map identifies where major legacy defects are addressed in the 2026 counsel-review draft.

| 2022 handbook issue | Legacy location | 2026 draft correction |
|---|---|---|
| No controlled-document version, approval, review date, or revision history | Cover and acknowledgment | **Document control & approval** plus mandatory release checklist and source manifest |
| Employee asked to “agree” to all handbook content | Pages 1, 33–34 | **Employee acknowledgment and receipt** uses receipt/non-waiver language and version binding |
| Paid sick leave limited to 24 hours / 3 days and 48-hour cap | Pages 25–26 | **Paid sick leave** uses current 40-hour / 5-day minimum guardrail and local-overlay release gate |
| Rigid 24-hour sick-call requirement and discipline threat | Page 7 | **Attendance, call-off, missed shifts, and continuity of care** distinguishes foreseeable and unforeseeable absence and protected leave |
| Blanket doctor-note rule after three days | Page 25 | **Paid sick leave** removes blanket certification as a condition of statutory sick leave |
| Overnight shift example splits time at midnight to defeat overtime | Page 18 | **Overtime, workday, workweek, and schedule controls** requires a fixed lawful workday/workweek and prohibits manipulation |
| Meal period described mainly for facility work; field breaks left to “judgment” | Page 17 | **Meal periods, rest periods, lactation, and heat safety** applies wage-order rules to field work and creates missed-break reporting |
| No necessary-expense reimbursement policy | Omitted | **Expense reimbursement, mileage, travel, phones, and supplies** |
| 60%–70% PFL benefit and mandatory PTO-before-PFL language | Page 30 | **California Disability Insurance and Paid Family Leave** uses current benefit guardrails and prohibits mandatory PTO-before-PFL |
| “Paid Disability Leave (PDL)” terminology | Page 30 | **FMLA, CFRA, Pregnancy Disability Leave, and reasonable accommodation** correctly distinguishes PDL from wage replacement |
| CFRA, PDL, bereavement, reproductive-loss, and expanded violence-related leave incomplete or absent | Leave section | **Leave overview**, **FMLA/CFRA/PDL**, and **Other protected leave** sections |
| Medical marijuana treated as an illegal drug without current protections | Pages 13–14 | **Drug-free workplace, cannabis, alcohol, medication, smoking, and fitness for duty** |
| Modern workplace-violence prevention program absent | Safety section | **IIPP, workplace violence prevention, emergency preparedness, and hazard reporting** |
| Required 2026 Workplace Know Your Rights notice absent | Omitted | **Required notices, workplace postings, and access to rights information** |
| HHA 12-hour in-service requirement applied to all employees | Page 18 | **HHA supplement** limits the federal 12-hour rule to HHAs and separates other training types |
| HHA 14-day / 60-day supervision and direct observation omitted | Omitted | **Home Health Aide requirements, in-service education, competency, and supervision** |
| Bloodborne-pathogen policy limited largely to vaccine language | Pages 14–15 | **Bloodborne pathogens, ATD, infection prevention, and exposure response** |
| Abuse-reporting instruction vague and lacking external-report distinction | Page 12 | **Patient rights, abuse/neglect reporting, boundaries, and professional conduct** |
| Incident defined mainly as injury-producing accident | Page 8 | **Work-related injury, workers’ compensation, and incident reporting** covers clinical, privacy, safety, vehicle, and near-miss events |
| Generic confidentiality language insufficient for mobile home health | Pages 11–12 | **HIPAA, CMIA, confidentiality, and minimum necessary** plus **Technology, mobile devices, email, social media, and AI** |
| Employee monitoring language lacks modern privacy framework | Pages 15–16 | **Employee privacy, monitoring, and personnel records** |
| Holiday/benefit commitments may no longer match current plans | Pages 21, 32 | **Benefits, holidays, retirement, and plan documents** defers to current approved plan documents and schedules |
| Final pay/property language risks unlawful withholding | Pages 6 and 32 | **Pay, wage statements, minimum wage, and final pay** plus **Separation and offboarding** |
| “vacation or sick leave (remove)” editing note left in final document | Page 32 | Controlled source, QA, and approval workflow prevent editorial notes from becoming employee-facing policy |
| No complete employee lifecycle | Throughout | Appendix A maps pre-hire, Day 1, 30/60/90, ongoing, annual, leave/return, and separation |
| Policies and forms not linked | Throughout | Every section contains internal policy, form/record, owner, and external-authority references |
