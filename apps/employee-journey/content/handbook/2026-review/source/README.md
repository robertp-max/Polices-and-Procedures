# Care Indeed 2026 Employee Handbook Package

## Primary artifact

- `Care_Indeed_Employee_Field_Workforce_Handbook_2026_Counsel_Review_Draft.html` — self-contained, responsive, searchable, interactive, and print-ready handbook draft.

## Review and implementation files

- `Care_Indeed_Handbook_Policy_Form_Crosswalk.csv` — handbook section to internal policy/form/authority mapping.
- `HANDBOOK_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` — mandatory legal, operational, payroll, clinical, privacy, safety, and approval gates.
- `2022_TO_2026_REPLACEMENT_MAP.md` — major legacy defects and where they are corrected in the new draft.
- `HANDBOOK_UI_QA.md` — source, interaction, responsive, and accessibility validation.
- `HANDBOOK_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` — SHA-256 integrity and source counts.
- `PREVIEW_desktop.png` and `PREVIEW_mobile.png` — rendered design previews.

## Status

**Counsel-review draft. Not effective. Do not distribute for employee acknowledgment until every release-checklist item is complete.**

The handbook is deliberately marked as a controlled draft because employer-specific facts must still be confirmed, including legal entity, work locations, local ordinances, wage orders, health-care minimum-wage coverage, paid-leave plan design, benefit plan terms, contacts, screening practices, and role supplements.

## Coverage

The HTML contains:

- 48 handbook sections;
- 104 unique Care Indeed policy references;
- 52 unique Care Indeed form/record references;
- 25 official external source notes;
- a complete Employee Journey lifecycle map;
- policy and forms indexes;
- a mandatory release checklist;
- print CSS with a counsel-review watermark.

## Policy freshness warning

The supplied policy corpus contains 272 policies; 205 are past their recorded next-review date as of July 24, 2026, including 73 policies cited by this handbook. Those records require policy-owner reconciliation before release.

## Design

The HTML uses the Care Indeed Employee Journey visual language: warm ivory canvas, white surfaces, deep teal structure, dark orange actions, Montserrat-style headings, Roboto-style body copy, rounded premium cards, responsive mobile navigation, search, reading progress, policy/form reference panels, and print styling.
