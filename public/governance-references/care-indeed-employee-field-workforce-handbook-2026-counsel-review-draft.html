EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP

At-will employment and no-contract statement

Unless a written agreement signed by an expressly authorized officer states otherwise, employment is at will.

Either the employee or Care Indeed may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without advance notice and with or without cause, subject to applicable law. No supervisor or manager may make an oral promise of guaranteed employment or alter at-will status.

The at-will relationship does not permit discrimination, retaliation, wage violations, interference with protected leave, unlawful discipline, or termination for exercising a protected right. Progressive discipline may be used but is not a contractual prerequisite to termination.

Acknowledgment is receipt—not waiver
Signing the handbook acknowledgment confirms receipt and understanding. It does not waive statutory rights or convert policy summaries into an employment contract.

FAIR EMPLOYMENT

Equal employment opportunity, accommodations, and respectful work

Care Indeed prohibits discrimination and provides reasonable accommodation through a timely, good-faith interactive process.

Employment decisions are made without unlawful discrimination based on any characteristic protected by federal, California, or applicable local law, including race, color, ancestry, national origin, citizenship or immigration status where protected, religion, creed, sex, pregnancy, childbirth and related conditions, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, marital status, age, disability, medical condition, genetic information, reproductive health decision-making, military or veteran status, or any other protected status.

Applicants and employees may request reasonable accommodation for disability, medical condition, pregnancy-related needs, sincerely held religious belief or practice, or another protected reason. Requests may be oral or written. Care Indeed will engage in the interactive process and will seek only information reasonably necessary to evaluate the request.

  • Contact Human Resources or any manager to request accommodation.
  • Managers must promptly forward potential accommodation requests to Human Resources.
  • Medical information is maintained separately and shared only as permitted or necessary.
  • No retaliation is permitted for requesting or using an accommodation.
Emergency or immediate safety need
Tell a supervisor or Human Resources immediately if a temporary change is needed to work safely while the formal process is pending.

SAFE & RESPECTFUL WORKPLACE

Harassment prevention, complaint channels, and non-retaliation

Harassment, discrimination, bullying tied to a protected status, sexual harassment, and retaliation are prohibited.

The policy applies to conduct involving employees, applicants, interns, volunteers, contractors, vendors, patients, families, visitors, and third parties in offices, patient homes, vehicles, digital communications, training, work events, and any work-related setting.

An employee may report concerns to any supervisor or manager, Human Resources, Compliance, the Administrator, the Director of Nursing, or the confidential reporting channel. Employees are not required to report to a person involved in the concern. Managers must report received concerns to Human Resources promptly.

  • Reports will be addressed promptly, fairly, and as confidentially as practical. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
  • Interim safety or work measures may be used while a concern is reviewed.
  • Good-faith reporting, participation in an investigation, requesting accommodation, or opposing unlawful conduct is protected.
  • Knowingly false statements may be addressed, but an unsubstantiated good-faith report is not misconduct.
Training
Covered employees receive California-compliant harassment-prevention training at the required supervisor or nonsupervisor duration and cadence. A handbook acknowledgment is not a substitute for interactive training.

SPEAK-UP RIGHTS

Protected reporting, whistleblowing, and concerted activity

Nothing in Care Indeed policy prohibits lawful reporting, wage discussions, protected group activity, agency complaints, or cooperation with government investigations.

Employees may discuss wages, hours, benefits, staffing, safety, and working conditions; act together for mutual aid or protection; report suspected legal or policy violations; contact a government agency; and cooperate with an investigation, subject to lawful limits on disclosure of patient information and proprietary data.

Care Indeed prohibits retaliation, intimidation, immigration-related threats, schedule punishment, adverse assignments, surveillance, discipline, or other adverse action because an employee exercised a protected right in good faith.

WAGES

Pay, wage statements, minimum wage, and final pay

Care Indeed pays all wages when due, provides itemized wage statements, and applies the highest applicable state, local, or covered health-care wage floor.

Regular paydays and pay periods are identified in the current wage notice and payroll calendar. Employees should promptly review wage statements and report missing hours, rates, premiums, reimbursements, or deductions. No employee will be retaliated against for raising a good-faith pay concern.

California’s statewide minimum wage is $16.90 per hour as of January 1, 2026. A higher local minimum wage or health-care-worker minimum wage may apply depending on the employee, location, and covered facility. The current applicable rate—not a handbook snapshot—controls.

Final wages are paid within the time required by California law. Employees discharged by the company are generally owed final wages at termination; employees who resign without at least 72 hours’ notice are generally owed final wages within 72 hours. Accrued vested vacation or PTO is paid at separation where required; statutory paid sick leave is not paid out unless required by a more protective plan or law.

WAGES

Overtime, workday, workweek, and schedule controls

Nonexempt employees receive overtime and double time whenever required by the applicable wage order and law.

California’s general rule requires time-and-one-half after eight hours in a workday, after 40 hours in a workweek, and for the first eight hours on a seventh consecutive workday; double time generally applies after 12 hours in a workday and after eight hours on the seventh consecutive workday. Exceptions and alternative rules may apply to specific classifications.

A fixed workday and workweek are established by Payroll and may not be manipulated to avoid overtime. An overnight shift is not automatically split in a way that defeats overtime. Employees must obtain advance approval when feasible, but all overtime actually worked must be reported and paid.

Classification-specific supplement required
Home-care, personal-attendant, live-in, clinical-professional, office, and exempt classifications may be governed by different wage orders or exceptions. Before release, counsel must confirm the wage-order matrix for every Care Indeed role and work location.

WAGES & WELL-BEING

Meal periods, rest periods, lactation, and heat safety

Field work does not eliminate lawful meal and rest rights.

Nonexempt employees receive meal periods and paid rest periods under the applicable wage order. The general California rule provides a 30-minute meal period before the end of the fifth hour, a second meal period when required for longer shifts, and paid ten-minute net rest periods for each four hours or major fraction worked. Lawful waivers and on-duty meal agreements require specific conditions and, when required, a revocable written agreement.

Employees should report missed, late, short, interrupted, or on-duty meal/rest periods using the current payroll process. Care Indeed will investigate and pay any legally required premium. Employees must never falsely record an off-duty meal that was not provided.

Care Indeed provides lactation break time and a compliant private location, including required seating, surface, electricity, nearby water, and refrigeration. Field employees should contact HR before or during an assignment so a practical arrangement can be made.

Employees working in heat or other environmental hazards must follow the applicable prevention plan, hydration, rest, communication, and emergency procedures. No employee is expected to remain in an unsafe environment without escalation.

SCHEDULING

Attendance, call-off, missed shifts, and continuity of care

Employees must communicate absences promptly, while Care Indeed distinguishes unforeseeable illness and protected leave from avoidable attendance misconduct.

For foreseeable absences, employees should provide as much advance notice as practical. For unexpected illness, emergency, or protected leave, notify the designated staffing or supervisory contact as soon as practical. A rigid 24-hour requirement does not apply to an unforeseeable illness.

Field staff must not abandon a patient. If relief is delayed or the employee becomes ill on duty, immediately contact the supervisor or on-call service, remain clocked in, follow infection-control and safety instructions, and remain only to the extent safe and necessary until a qualified handoff or emergency response occurs.

  • No-call/no-show and repeated avoidable tardiness may lead to corrective action after review.
  • Protected sick leave, approved leave, disability accommodation, workers’ compensation, and other legally protected absences are not counted or disciplined unlawfully.
  • A missed visit or late arrival must be reported and documented under clinical and scheduling policy.

LEAVE & WAGES

Paid sick leave

Care Indeed provides paid sick leave that meets or exceeds California and applicable local requirements.

At minimum, eligible California employees may use no less than 40 hours or five days of paid sick leave per year, whichever provides more time, unless a more protective local law or company plan applies. The current plan’s accrual or frontload method, benefit year, cap, and balance are shown in the wage notice, pay statement, or employee portal.

Paid sick leave may be used for diagnosis, care, treatment, or preventive care for the employee, a qualifying family member, or a designated person; and for qualifying safe-time purposes. Requests may be oral or written. Care Indeed will not require employees to find a replacement or retaliate for lawful use.

Foreseeable use should be reported in advance when practical; unforeseeable use should be reported as soon as practical. Medical documentation is not a blanket condition of using statutory paid sick leave, though documentation may be requested when permitted under a different applicable leave law or where there is a legitimate, legally supportable basis.

Local overlay
Bay Area ordinances may provide additional accrual, use, notice, or supplemental-pay rights. The release checklist requires a location-by-location overlay before this handbook becomes effective.
