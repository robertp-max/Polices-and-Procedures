## 2. Purpose

This policy establishes the formal organizational hierarchy, reporting relationships, and lines of authority for all agency operations. It ensures that all personnel, contracted staff, and leadership roles operate within a clearly defined structure that supports accountability, supervisory oversight, regulatory compliance, and effective delivery of patient care services. This policy satisfies requirements under 42 CFR § 484.105 — Condition of Participation: Organization and Administration of Services.

---

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All agency employees (full-time, part-time, and per-diem)
- All contracted clinical and administrative staff
- Volunteer personnel
- Agency leadership including the Administrator, Clinical Manager, and Compliance Officer
- All service lines and operational departments within the agency

---

## 4. Policy Statement

4.1 The agency shall maintain and operate under a formally documented organizational structure that defines all reporting relationships, lines of authority, and supervisory responsibilities at all levels of the organization.

4.2 The organizational structure shall be depicted in an official Organizational Chart (Appendix A) that is reviewed and approved by the Governing Body annually and updated within 14 calendar days of any structural change.

4.3 The Governing Body holds ultimate authority over the organizational structure and delegates operational authority to the Administrator, who in turn directs all agency departments and service lines.

4.4 Clinical authority for patient care services shall be delegated to the Clinical Manager (Director of Nursing), who reports directly to the Administrator and is accountable to the Governing Body for all clinical operations.

4.5 No individual shall hold a position without a defined supervisor and without having received a written job description specifying their role, responsibilities, qualifications, and reporting relationship, per policy HR-HR-001.

4.6 The agency shall not permit organizational arrangements that create unauthorized dual-authority conflicts, undefined reporting lines, or situations where clinical oversight is not clearly assigned.

4.7 All organizational structure changes affecting key leadership roles (Administrator, Clinical Manager, Compliance Officer, or department heads) must be approved by the Governing Body before implementation, per policy GV-GA-001.

4.8 Only the most current approved version of this policy and its associated Organizational Chart shall be considered valid. Superseded versions must not be used for any operational or compliance purpose.

## 5. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| Organizational Chart | A visual representation of the agency's formal hierarchy, reporting lines, and departmental structure. |
| Line of Authority | The formal chain of command from the Governing Body through the Administrator to individual employees. |
| Direct Report | An employee who is formally assigned to and supervised by a specific supervisor or manager. |
| Span of Control | The number of direct reports assigned to a single supervisor. The agency shall maintain spans of control consistent with clinical safety and operational effectiveness. |
| Clinical Manager | The registered nurse designated by the Governing Body to supervise and direct all clinical services, as required by 42 CFR § 484.105(c). |
| Dual-Authority Conflict | A situation in which an employee receives conflicting direction from two supervisors with overlapping or undefined authority. |

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 Establishing and Documenting the Organizational Structure

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
|:-----|:-----------------|:-------|:----------|
| 6.1.1 | Administrator | Develop and maintain the agency's official Organizational Chart (Appendix A) reflecting all current positions, reporting relationships, and lines of authority. | Initial chart developed prior to agency operation; maintained continuously. |
| 6.1.2 | Administrator | Submit the updated Organizational Chart to the Governing Body for formal review and approval at the first quarterly meeting of each calendar year. | Annually at Q1 Governing Body meeting. |
| 6.1.3 | Administrator | Update the Organizational Chart within 14 calendar days of any structural change including: (a) creation or elimination of a position; (b) change in reporting relationship; (c) appointment or removal of leadership personnel; (d) addition of a new service line. | Within 14 calendar days of any structural change. |
| 6.1.4 | Administrator | Distribute the current approved Organizational Chart to all department heads and make it accessible to all staff through the agency's policy portal or posted common area. | Within 7 calendar days of each approved version. |
| 6.1.5 | Human Resources / Administrator | Ensure every position on the Organizational Chart has a corresponding current written job description on file, per policy HR-HR-001. | Continuous; reviewed annually or when job duties change. |

### 6.2 Governing Body and Executive Leadership Structure

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
|:-----|:-----------------|:-------|:----------|
| 6.2.1 | Governing Body | Retain ultimate authority at the top of the organizational hierarchy. The Administrator and all agency operations report ultimately to the Governing Body through formal governance mechanisms (quarterly meetings, reports, directives). | Continuous. |
| 6.2.2 | Governing Body | Formally designate the Administrator as the operational head of the agency. All department heads, the Clinical Manager, and the Compliance Officer shall report to the Administrator unless otherwise specified in Governing Body-approved structure. | Per Governing Body appointment resolution. |
| 6.2.3 | Administrator | Ensure that the Clinical Manager reports directly to the Administrator and that no other department head or supervisor directs clinical care activities without the Clinical Manager's oversight and concurrence. | Continuous. |
| 6.2.4 | Administrator | Ensure that the Compliance Officer has functional independence with direct reporting access to the Governing Body and the Administrator, as required by policy CO-CR-002. | Continuous. |

### 6.3 Department Structure and Reporting Relationships

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
|:-----|:-----------------|:-------|:----------|
| 6.3.1 | Administrator | Define and document the agency's departmental structure, which must include at minimum: (a) Clinical Services (supervised by Clinical Manager); (b) Human Resources; (c) Finance/Billing; (d) Compliance; (e) Intake/Scheduling; (f) Quality Assurance (if separate from clinical). | At organizational setup; reviewed annually. |
| 6.3.2 | Clinical Manager | Maintain a current staffing structure for all clinical personnel including: nurses, therapists, aides, and social workers. Assign clear supervisory responsibility for each clinical discipline. | Continuous; updated within 7 calendar days of any clinical staffing change. |
| 6.3.3 | Department Heads | Each department head is responsible for: (a) maintaining a current list of their direct reports; (b) ensuring each direct report has a current job description; (c) conducting and documenting annual performance evaluations per policy HR-HR-004. | Continuous; performance evaluations annually. |
| 6.3.4 | Administrator | Evaluate spans of control at least annually and address any situation where a supervisor's span of control creates a risk to patient safety, staff oversight, or operational integrity. | Annually; at Q1 operational review. |

### 6.4 Contracted and Temporary Staff Integration

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
|:-----|:-----------------|:-------|:----------|
| 6.4.1 | Administrator / Clinical Manager | Identify the agency supervisor responsible for oversight of each contracted clinical or administrative service. Supervisory assignments must be documented in the contract or service agreement per policy GV-GA-010. | Prior to contract execution and at each renewal. |
| 6.4.2 | Clinical Manager | Ensure contracted clinical staff operate under the same clinical supervision structure as agency employees. Contracted clinicians must receive direction from the assigned supervising clinician and report patient status through established clinical communication channels. | Continuous. |
| 6.4.3 | Administrator | Ensure the Organizational Chart includes reference to contracted service lines and identifies the agency supervisor responsible for oversight, even if individual contract staff are not named. | At each organizational chart update. |

### 6.5 Communication of Organizational Structure to Staff

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
|:-----|:-----------------|:-------|:----------|
| 6.5.1 | Human Resources / Administrator | Communicate the reporting structure to each new employee during orientation, per policy HR-HR-002. Each employee must sign and date acknowledgment of their job description and reporting relationship. | At hire; prior to patient care assignment. |
| 6.5.2 | Department Heads | Address any employee inquiry or confusion regarding reporting lines, supervision, or authority within 3 business days. Unresolved structural ambiguities must be escalated to the Administrator. | Within 3 business days of identification. |
| 6.5.3 | Administrator | Communicate structural changes affecting staff to all affected personnel within 7 calendar days of change implementation, via written notice or staff meeting. | Within 7 calendar days of any structural change. |

### 6.6 Escalation and Exception Handling

| Condition | Escalation Path | Corrective Action | Timeframe |
|:----------|:---------------|:-----------------|:----------|
| Position on Organizational Chart with no identified supervisor | Department Head → Administrator | Administrator identifies and documents supervisor within 3 business days. | Within 3 business days. |
| Employee without a written job description | HR → Administrator | HR issues a written job description within 7 calendar days. Position suspended from patient care assignment until job description is in place. | Within 7 calendar days. |
| Dual-authority conflict identified by employee | Supervisor → Administrator | Administrator clarifies and documents the reporting relationship in writing. Governing Body notified if the conflict involves a leadership-level position. | Within 5 business days of report. |
| Organizational Chart not updated within 14 days of structural change | Administrator → Governing Body | Compliance Officer documents the deficiency. Administrator updates the chart immediately. | Immediate upon identification. |
| Clinical staff without identified clinical supervisor | Clinical Manager → Administrator | Clinical Manager immediately assigns a supervisor and documents the assignment. Patient care assignment suspended until resolved. | Immediate; within 24 hours of identification. |

## 8. Compliance & Audit Considerations

**8.1 CMS Survey Focus Areas**

CMS surveyors will review:
- Current Organizational Chart showing all positions and reporting lines.
- Evidence that the Clinical Manager reports directly to the Administrator and that all clinical staff have identified supervisors.
- Job descriptions on file for all staff assessed during the survey.
- Governing Body approval of organizational structure documented in meeting minutes.

**8.2 Common Deficiency Areas**
- Organizational Chart is outdated or does not reflect actual staff structure.
- Clinical staff do not know who their supervisor is.
- Contracted staff have no identified agency supervisor.
- Job descriptions are missing, unsigned, or do not match actual duties performed.
- No evidence that Governing Body approved the organizational structure.

**8.3 Internal Audit Indicators**
- Organizational Chart approved by Governing Body within past 12 months: YES / NO
- All positions on chart have an identified supervisor: YES / NO
- All employees have a signed job description on file: YES / NO
- All clinical staff can identify their supervising clinician: YES / NO
