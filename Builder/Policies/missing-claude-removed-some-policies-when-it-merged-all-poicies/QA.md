QA-PG-001 — QAPI Program Establishment & Governance

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes the agency's Quality Assessment and Performance Improvement (QAPI) program as a formalized, data-driven, and governing body–directed system for continuous quality monitoring, performance improvement, and patient safety assurance at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The QAPI program is the central mechanism through which the agency identifies opportunities for improvement, implements evidence-based interventions, monitors outcomes, and sustains gains across all operational and clinical domains. This policy ensures the agency satisfies the requirements set forth in 42 CFR § 484.65 — Condition of Participation: Quality Assessment and Performance Improvement, which mandates that a home health agency must develop, implement, evaluate, and maintain an effective, ongoing, agency-wide, data-driven QAPI program.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The Governing Body of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc.

The Agency Administrator

The QAPI Coordinator (designated program lead)

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

All members of the QAPI Committee

The Compliance Officer

All clinical, administrative, and support staff whose work contributes to or is measured by the QAPI program

All contracted and per diem staff providing services on behalf of the agency

Exclusion: This policy does not govern the detailed methodology of individual Performance Improvement Projects (PIPs), which is addressed in QA-PI-001, nor adverse event investigation procedures, which are addressed in QA-AE-001 and QA-AE-002. This policy establishes the governance framework within which those activities operate.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall establish, maintain, and continuously operate an agency-wide QAPI program that reflects the complexity of the agency's organization and services, as required by 42 CFR § 484.65(a).

4.2 The QAPI program shall be governed by the Governing Body, which retains ultimate accountability for the program's effectiveness, resource allocation, and strategic direction. The Governing Body shall not delegate this ultimate accountability.

4.3 The Governing Body shall designate a qualified QAPI Coordinator with defined authority, dedicated time, and sufficient resources to administer the QAPI program on a day-to-day basis.

4.4 The QAPI program shall be data-driven, utilizing measurable indicators derived from clinical outcomes, patient safety events, operational performance, patient experience, regulatory compliance, and financial data to identify priorities and evaluate improvement interventions.

4.5 The QAPI program shall integrate and coordinate with the agency's compliance program (CO-CP-001), risk management program (RM-ER-001), infection prevention and control program (CL-SD-016), and all clinical and operational domains to ensure a unified quality and safety framework.

4.6 The QAPI program shall maintain a written QAPI plan, approved annually by the Governing Body per policy QA-PG-002, that defines the program's goals, measurable indicators, performance improvement project requirements, committee structure, reporting schedule, and accountability framework.

4.7 All agency staff — clinical, administrative, and contracted — are responsible for participating in quality improvement activities within their scope of practice and role, and for reporting quality concerns, adverse events, and near-misses through established channels.

4.8 The QAPI program shall comply with all applicable CMS Conditions of Participation, state regulatory requirements, and accreditation standards. The agency shall not operate any service line or clinical program outside the scope of QAPI monitoring.

4.9 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid. Superseded versions must not be used for any operational or compliance purpose. Any revision to this policy requires Governing Body approval and re-acknowledgment by all personnel within scope within 14 calendar days of the revised effective date.

8.2 Surveyor Expectations

CMS surveyors conducting a standard survey under SOM Appendix B will specifically verify:

Evidence that a QAPI program exists and functions. Surveyors will request the QAPI plan, QAPI Program Description, and committee meeting minutes. An agency that cannot produce these documents will face an immediate Condition-level inquiry under 42 CFR § 484.65.

Evidence that the QAPI program is agency-wide. Surveyors will verify that all service lines — not just nursing — are monitored by the QAPI program. A program limited to a single discipline will be cited as non-compliant.

Evidence that the QAPI program is data-driven. Surveyors will look for measurable indicators, dashboards, trending data, and documented analysis. Programs that rely solely on anecdotal discussion without data will be cited.

Evidence that the Governing Body exercises oversight. Surveyors will cross-reference QAPI Committee reports with Governing Body minutes to verify that the Governing Body receives, reviews, discusses, and acts upon quality data. Passive receipt of reports without documented discussion or action is a common deficiency.

Evidence that performance improvement projects exist. Surveyors will request documentation of active and completed PIPs including methodology, data, and results. An agency with no active PIPs will be cited.

Evidence that adverse events are identified, reported, and acted upon. Surveyors will review the adverse event reporting process, investigation records, and corrective action documentation.

Evidence that the QAPI program leads to measurable improvement. Surveyors will look for documented improvement trends, not just identification of problems. A program that identifies issues but shows no improvement over time will be cited for ineffectiveness.

QA-PG-002 — QAPI Plan Development & Annual Review

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy mandates the development, content requirements, approval process, and annual review of a written QAPI plan for Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The QAPI plan is the agency's actionable roadmap for quality improvement — translating the governance structure established in QA-PG-001 into defined goals, measurable indicators, performance improvement priorities, committee responsibilities, and reporting timelines for each calendar year. This policy ensures the agency satisfies the requirements of 42 CFR § 484.65(a) and (e), which require the governing body to ensure the QAPI program has a written plan with defined goals and measurable outcomes, and that the governing body approves the plan and ensures adequate resources for its execution.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The Governing Body of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc.

The Agency Administrator

The QAPI Coordinator

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The Compliance Officer

All department heads and supervisors responsible for achieving QAPI plan goals

Exclusion: This policy defines QAPI plan content, approval, and review requirements. It does not govern the execution of individual PIPs (QA-PI-001) or specific quality indicator monitoring procedures (QA-PI-002), though the QAPI plan must define priorities for both.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a written QAPI plan that is reviewed, updated, and approved by the Governing Body annually, at the first quarterly meeting of each calendar year.

4.2 The QAPI plan shall define the agency's quality improvement priorities for the plan year based on data analysis of the prior year's performance, regulatory requirements, patient population characteristics, and identified areas of risk.

4.3 The QAPI plan shall include measurable goals with defined performance thresholds, responsible parties, monitoring frequency, and expected outcomes for each priority area.

4.4 The QAPI plan shall identify a minimum of two (2) active Performance Improvement Projects (PIPs) at all times, proportionate to the scope and complexity of the agency's services, as required by 42 CFR § 484.65(d).

4.5 The QAPI plan shall not be treated as a static document. The QAPI Coordinator shall update the plan as needed throughout the year in response to: (a) new adverse event trends; (b) significant regulatory changes; (c) CMS survey findings; (d) Governing Body directives. All mid-year modifications must be documented, approved by the QAPI Committee, and reported to the Governing Body at the next quarterly meeting.

4.6 The QAPI plan year shall align with the calendar year (January 1 through December 31). The plan for the upcoming year must be finalized and submitted to the Governing Body for approval no later than December 15 of the preceding year.

QA-PI-001 — Performance Improvement Project Management

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for selecting, designing, implementing, monitoring, and documenting Performance Improvement Projects (PIPs) at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. PIPs are the primary mechanism through which the agency translates quality data analysis into targeted, measurable improvement in specific areas of clinical care, operational performance, or patient safety. This policy ensures the agency satisfies 42 CFR § 484.65(c) and (d), which require the agency to conduct performance improvement activities focused on high-priority areas, implement and track PIPs proportionate to the agency's scope and complexity, and demonstrate measurable improvement over time.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The QAPI Coordinator

All QAPI Committee members

All PIP leads and team members

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The Administrator

All department heads whose areas are the subject of a PIP

All staff participating in PIP data collection, intervention, or implementation activities

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a minimum of two (2) active PIPs at all times, proportionate to the scope and complexity of the agency's services, as required by 42 CFR § 484.65(d).

4.2 At least one active PIP shall address a clinical outcome or patient safety topic. The second PIP may address clinical, operational, compliance, or patient experience areas.

4.3 PIPs shall be selected based on data analysis identifying areas of underperformance, adverse trends, high-risk processes, or areas with the greatest potential for patient impact. PIP selection shall not be arbitrary.

4.4 Each PIP shall follow a structured quality improvement methodology (Plan-Do-Study-Act [PDSA], or equivalent evidence-based methodology approved by the QAPI Committee) and shall include: (a) a defined problem statement based on data; (b) measurable goals with numeric targets; (c) root cause analysis or contributing factor analysis; (d) specific, implementable interventions; (e) defined data collection plan; (f) monitoring schedule; (g) evaluation criteria; (h) sustainment plan.

4.5 Each PIP shall have a designated PIP Lead who is accountable for project execution, data collection, reporting, and achievement of measurable goals. The PIP Lead shall report progress to the QAPI Committee monthly.

4.6 PIP results shall be documented and reported to the Governing Body through the quarterly QAPI Performance Report. Completed PIPs shall include a final evaluation with demonstrated outcomes and sustainment plan.

4.7 PIPs that fail to demonstrate measurable progress within the defined evaluation timeframe shall be subject to reassessment by the QAPI Committee, including consideration of methodology revision, resource reallocation, or project redesign.

4.8 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

QA-PI-002 — Quality Indicator Monitoring & Reporting

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes the agency's quality indicator dashboard, monitoring frequency, data analysis methodology, performance thresholds, reporting requirements, and escalation triggers for adverse performance trends at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. Quality indicators are the quantifiable measures through which the QAPI program tracks agency performance, identifies improvement opportunities, and evaluates the impact of interventions. This policy ensures compliance with 42 CFR § 484.65(b), which requires the agency to use quality indicator data, including measures derived from OASIS, to monitor the effectiveness of care and identify opportunities for improvement.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The QAPI Coordinator

All QAPI Committee members

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The Administrator

All department heads and supervisors responsible for data collection or indicator performance

All clinical staff whose work generates indicator data

The Compliance Officer

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a defined set of quality indicators organized into a Quality Indicator Dashboard that is reviewed at each QAPI Committee meeting and reported to the Governing Body quarterly.

4.2 Quality indicators shall span the following domains at minimum: (a) clinical outcomes; (b) patient safety; (c) process compliance; (d) patient experience; (e) utilization and efficiency; (f) workforce competency.

4.3 Each quality indicator shall have a defined performance threshold. Thresholds shall be established using: (a) national benchmarks (CMS Home Health Compare, HHCAHPS national averages); (b) state comparative data; (c) agency historical performance; or (d) regulatory standards. Thresholds shall be reviewed and updated annually during QAPI plan development.

4.4 Any quality indicator that falls below its defined threshold for 2 consecutive reporting periods shall trigger mandatory corrective action analysis by the QAPI Committee, documented in committee minutes.

4.5 Any quality indicator that falls below its defined threshold for 3 consecutive reporting periods without documented improvement shall be escalated to the Governing Body per QA-PG-001 Section 6.5.

4.6 Quality indicator data shall be trended over time (minimum 12-month rolling trend) to identify patterns, seasonal variations, and the impact of improvement interventions.

4.7 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

QA-PI-004 — Data-Driven Decision Making

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy requires that all quality improvement initiatives, operational changes, and resource allocation decisions at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. be supported by documented data analysis rather than anecdotal observation, assumption, or convenience. Data-driven decision making is the foundational principle of the QAPI program and ensures that the agency's limited resources are directed toward interventions with the greatest evidence of impact. This policy operationalizes 42 CFR § 484.65(b), which requires the use of quality indicator data to monitor and improve care effectiveness.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The Governing Body

The Administrator

The QAPI Coordinator and QAPI Committee

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

All department heads and supervisors who propose or approve operational, clinical, or quality improvement changes

The Compliance Officer

4. Policy Statements

4.1 All quality improvement decisions — including PIP selection, corrective action development, policy modifications based on quality concerns, resource reallocation, process changes, and staff education priorities — shall be supported by documented data analysis.

4.2 Decisions presented to the QAPI Committee or Governing Body for approval must include: (a) the data source(s); (b) the analysis performed; (c) the findings; (d) the recommended action with rationale linked to findings.

4.3 Anecdotal observations and individual experiences are valued as input for generating hypotheses and identifying potential areas of concern. However, they shall not serve as the sole basis for resource allocation, process changes, or corrective actions without supporting data analysis.

4.4 The agency shall maintain data literacy among all QAPI Committee members and department heads through training on data interpretation, basic statistical concepts, and evidence-based decision-making practices.

4.5 Data integrity is a prerequisite for data-driven decisions. The agency shall ensure data accuracy, completeness, and timeliness through defined quality controls per CO-DC-001 and CL-OA-005.

4.6 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

5. Definitions

6. Procedures

6.1 Decision Criteria and Documentation Requirements

6.2 Data Analysis Standards

6.3 Data Literacy and Training

6.4 Escalation

7. Documentation Requirements

8. Compliance Monitoring

8.2 Surveyor Expectations

Surveyors will: (1) ask how the agency uses data to drive quality improvement; (2) review QAPI Committee minutes for evidence that data analysis precedes decisions; (3) look for documented data analysis in PIP charters and corrective action plans; (4) assess whether decisions are data-driven or anecdote-driven.

QA-PI-006 — Visit Utilization & LUPA Risk Management Program

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes a cross-functional QAPI program integrating clinical, scheduling, and financial data to monitor visit utilization patterns, identify Low Utilization Payment Adjustment (LUPA) risk episodes early, and implement coordinated interventions to ensure physician-ordered services are delivered as planned at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. LUPA episodes — those where the number of visits delivered falls below the CMS-defined threshold — represent both a clinical risk (patients not receiving ordered care) and a financial risk (reduced reimbursement). This policy ensures that visit utilization is managed as a quality of care issue, not merely a billing concern, consistent with 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program scope), 42 CFR § 484.60 (care planning and coordination), and 42 CFR § 484.55 (comprehensive assessment).

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The QAPI Coordinator

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The Administrator

The CFO / Revenue Cycle Director

All QAPI Committee members

All scheduling and intake staff

All clinical staff responsible for visit delivery (RNs, LVNs, therapists, MSWs, HHAs)

All clinical supervisors

Exclusion: This policy governs the QAPI monitoring and intervention framework for visit utilization. Individual billing procedures for LUPA episodes are governed by FN-BC-001 and FN-CM-005. Clinical visit frequency determination is governed by CL-CP-001 and CL-SD-025.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a structured, data-driven visit utilization monitoring program integrated within the QAPI framework that identifies episodes at risk of LUPA and coordinates clinical, scheduling, and financial interventions to ensure ordered services are delivered.

4.2 Visit utilization monitoring shall be treated as a patient care quality issue. Failure to deliver physician-ordered visits is a care delivery deficiency that affects clinical outcomes, not merely a revenue concern.

4.3 The agency shall maintain a real-time or near-real-time LUPA risk dashboard that identifies episodes approaching the LUPA visit threshold, enabling proactive intervention before the episode closes.

4.4 A cross-functional LUPA Prevention Team — comprising clinical, scheduling, and revenue cycle representatives — shall be designated to review LUPA risk episodes weekly and coordinate interventions.

4.5 All LUPA episodes that close shall be subject to a retrospective root cause analysis to determine whether the LUPA resulted from: (a) a clinically appropriate low-utilization case; (b) a scheduling failure; (c) a patient-driven factor (refusal, hospitalization, no-show); (d) a clinician-driven factor (missed visits, documentation gaps); or (e) a system failure (staffing shortage, communication breakdown).

4.6 LUPA rate shall be a standing quality indicator on the QAPI dashboard per QA-PI-002 with a defined performance threshold, and shall be reported to the Governing Body quarterly.

QA-AE-002 — Root Cause Analysis Process

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes the methodology, team composition, documentation requirements, and completion standards for conducting Root Cause Analyses (RCAs) at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. An RCA is a structured, retrospective investigative process used to identify the fundamental systemic cause(s) of adverse events, sentinel events, or persistent quality failures — moving beyond the immediate or apparent cause to uncover the underlying process, system, or organizational factors that allowed the event to occur. RCA is mandatory for all Level 4 (Serious Harm) and Level 5 (Sentinel Event) adverse events per QA-AE-001, and may be directed by the QAPI Committee for any event or pattern deemed significant. This policy supports compliance with 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program effectiveness and data use) and 42 CFR § 484.60 (quality of care).

4.2 The QAPI Committee may direct an RCA for any event, pattern, or systemic failure it determines warrants formal root cause investigation, regardless of severity classification.

4.3 RCAs shall be conducted by a multidisciplinary team using a structured methodology that focuses on systems and processes, not individual blame.

4.4 Each RCA must produce identified root cause(s), contributing factors, and a Corrective Action Plan (per QA-AE-003) with measurable outcomes and defined accountability.

4.5 RCAs shall be completed within 45 calendar days of event identification. For sentinel events, the preliminary findings must be available to the Governing Body within 14 calendar days.

QA-AE-003 — Corrective Action Plan Development & Tracking

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for developing, implementing, tracking, verifying effectiveness, and closing Corrective Action Plans (CAPs) at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. A CAP is the structured, documented response to identified deficiencies — whether arising from adverse events, root cause analyses, quality indicator thresholds, CMS survey findings, compliance audits, or QAPI Committee directives. This policy ensures that identified problems are addressed with specific, measurable, accountable, and time-bound corrective actions, and that the agency can demonstrate to CMS surveyors and the Governing Body that quality improvement is sustained. This policy supports compliance with 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program improvement activities) and 42 CFR § 484.100 (compliance with federal, state, and local laws).

The Compliance Officer

The Risk Manager

4. Policy Statements

4.2 Each CAP must include: (a) a clear problem statement; (b) the identified root cause or contributing factors; (c) specific corrective actions; (d) responsible party for each action; (e) measurable outcome targets; (f) implementation timeline; (g) monitoring plan; (h) effectiveness verification criteria.

QA-SM-001 — Utilization Review & Management

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes a structured, data-driven Utilization Review (UR) program at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. that monitors the appropriateness, efficiency, and clinical necessity of all home health services delivered to patients. The UR program ensures that services are ordered and delivered based on documented clinical need, that visit frequency and duration align with patient goals and physician orders, and that patterns of over-utilization or under-utilization are identified and corrected. This policy satisfies the requirements of 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI — utilization data as a quality input), 42 CFR § 484.60 (care planning and quality of care), and 42 CFR § 484.55 (comprehensive assessment of patients), while also addressing the financial integrity requirements implicit in CMS payment integrity programs. Utilization review is treated as a quality of care program, not solely a cost-containment function.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The QAPI Coordinator

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The Administrator

The CFO / Revenue Cycle Director

Exclusion: Individual billing procedures for specific payers are governed by FN-BC-001. LUPA-specific monitoring procedures are governed by QA-PI-006 and FN-CM-005. This policy governs the overarching UR program framework.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a formal, agency-wide Utilization Review program that monitors the appropriateness, frequency, duration, and clinical justification of all home health services at the episode, discipline, visit, and clinician level.

QA-SM-003 — Patient Satisfaction Survey & Analysis

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes the agency's patient satisfaction survey and analysis program at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc., integrating the federally mandated Home Health Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems (HHCAHPS) survey with any supplemental satisfaction monitoring the agency conducts. Patient experience data reflects the patient's perspective on care quality and is a critical, non-clinical dimension of quality that complements clinical outcome monitoring. This policy ensures that HHCAHPS and supplemental data are systematically collected, analyzed, trended, and used to drive improvements in patient experience, communication, care delivery, and agency reputation. This policy satisfies the requirements of 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program — patient experience data as a quality input) and CMS HHCAHPS program requirements.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The QAPI Coordinator

The Administrator

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager

The CFO / Revenue Cycle Director (for Star Rating implications)

The Compliance Officer

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall comply with all CMS HHCAHPS program requirements including patient eligibility criteria, vendor contracting standards, and data submission timelines.

4.2 HHCAHPS results — including all reported measures, composite scores, and Star Ratings — shall be systematically monitored, trended, and reported through the QAPI program.

4.3 The agency shall supplement HHCAHPS data with agency-administered satisfaction monitoring (e.g., discharge satisfaction calls, post-discharge surveys) to obtain more timely, actionable feedback than the HHCAHPS quarterly release cycle provides.

4.4 Patient satisfaction data shall be a standing item on the monthly QAPI Committee agenda and shall be reported to the Governing Body quarterly.

4.5 When any HHCAHPS composite measure falls below the national average for 2 consecutive quarters, the QAPI Committee shall direct a documented root cause analysis and corrective action plan per QA-AE-003.

QA-SM-004 — Home Health Compare & Star Rating Monitoring

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes systematic monitoring, analysis, and response procedures for the agency's Home Health Compare publicly reported quality measures and Star Ratings at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. Home Health Compare data represents the agency's public quality profile — the information that patients, caregivers, referral sources, payers, and surveyors use to evaluate and compare the agency. Declining Star Ratings and below-average publicly reported quality measures have direct impacts on referral volume, competitive positioning, Governing Body accountability, and regulatory scrutiny. This policy ensures that the agency continuously monitors its public quality profile, understands the drivers of its ratings, and implements targeted improvement initiatives when ratings decline or fall below acceptable levels. This policy satisfies the requirements of 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program data use including publicly reported measures) and supports the benchmarking requirements of QA-PI-003.

QA-SM-005 — Policy Effectiveness Monitoring and Outcome Validation

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy requires Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. to systematically measure whether its policies are achieving their intended outcomes through defined compliance metrics, outcome indicators, and incident correlation analysis. Policies are the foundational mechanism through which the agency operationalizes regulatory requirements, best practices, and quality standards. A policy that exists in writing but does not change behavior or achieve measurable outcomes provides no quality assurance value and may create false compliance assurance. This policy ensures that the agency's policy framework is evaluated not merely for existence and acknowledgment, but for actual effectiveness — measured through outcomes data, compliance audit results, incident trends, and staff behavior indicators. This policy supports compliance with 42 CFR § 484.65 (QAPI program data-driven improvement) and the agency's enterprise policy governance framework per EN-TG-001.
