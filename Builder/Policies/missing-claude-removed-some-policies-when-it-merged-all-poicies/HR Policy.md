# HR Policy



POLICY HR-TA-001

Recruitment & Hiring Standards

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes standardized requirements for the recruitment, screening, and hiring of all employees and contracted staff at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The policy ensures that only qualified, eligible, and competent individuals are employed by the agency in compliance with 42 CFR § 484.105 (organization and administration), 42 CFR § 484.80 (home health aide qualifications), applicable California home health licensing requirements, OIG Compliance Program Guidance for Home Health Agencies, and all federal and state equal employment opportunity laws. A structured, documented, non-discriminatory hiring process protects the agency from regulatory liability, ensures patient safety, and promotes workforce quality.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

The HR Director and all HR department staff involved in recruitment and hiring

The Administrator

The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager (for clinical position hiring decisions)

All hiring managers and supervisors who participate in candidate evaluation or selection

All contracted staffing agencies providing personnel to Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc.

All applicants for employment or contracted engagement with the agency

This policy does not apply to: Governing Body member appointments, which are governed by policy GV-GB-001 and the agency's bylaws or operating agreement. Volunteer recruitment is governed by HR-WM-006.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain a standardized, documented recruitment and hiring process that ensures all candidates are evaluated based on objective, job-related criteria as defined in current job descriptions (HR-JD series) and applicable regulatory requirements.

4.2 All hiring decisions shall comply with federal and state equal employment opportunity laws, including Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA), and the California Fair Employment and Housing Act (FEHA). No applicant shall be discriminated against based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

4.3 No individual shall be employed or contracted by the agency without completion of all pre-employment screening requirements defined in this policy, including but not limited to: identity verification, licensure/certification verification (HR-TA-004), criminal background check (HR-TA-002), OIG/SAM exclusion screening (HR-TA-003), and reference verification.

4.4 All positions shall have a current, approved job description (HR-TA-006; HR-JD series) on file prior to posting or recruitment for that position. No position shall be filled without a job description that defines minimum qualifications, essential functions, and regulatory requirements.

4.5 The agency shall not employ or contract with any individual who is excluded from participation in any federal healthcare program, as verified through OIG LEIE and SAM database screening per policy HR-TA-003.

4.6 All recruitment and hiring documentation shall be maintained in accordance with record retention policy CO-HP-007, with personnel file standards per HR-WM-005 and HR-WM-007.

4.7 The agency shall maintain a log of all recruitment activity sufficient to demonstrate non-discriminatory hiring practices in the event of audit, complaint, or litigation.

4.8 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid. Superseded versions must not be used for any operational or compliance purpose.

POLICY HR-TA-002

Criminal Background Check & Screening

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes mandatory requirements for conducting criminal background checks on all prospective and current employees, contractors, and volunteers of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. Criminal background screening is essential to: (a) protect the safety and welfare of patients receiving home health services in their residences; (b) comply with California Health and Safety Code requirements for home health agency personnel; (c) satisfy CMS Conditions of Participation requirements for employing qualified and suitable staff; (d) fulfill the agency's duty of care and fiduciary obligations; and (e) mitigate risk of liability arising from negligent hiring. This policy ensures that background screening is conducted consistently, documented completely, and applied in compliance with the California Fair Chance Act and all applicable federal and state fair employment laws.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

All applicants for employment (full-time, part-time, per diem) at any stage after conditional offer

All contracted/staffing agency personnel assigned to work at or on behalf of the agency

All current employees subject to periodic rescreening as defined in this policy

All volunteers per HR-WM-006

The HR Director and all personnel responsible for initiating, reviewing, or acting upon background check results

This policy does not apply to: Governing Body members (screened per GV-GB-001 and HR-TA-003 for exclusion only, unless a Governing Body member also serves in an employment capacity). OIG/SAM exclusion screening is governed separately by HR-TA-003 but is coordinated with this policy.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall conduct a criminal background check on every individual prior to their performing any work duties for the agency, including orientation, patient contact, or access to patient information.

4.2 Background checks shall be conducted through an agency-approved, FCRA-compliant (Fair Credit Reporting Act) background screening vendor and shall include, at minimum: (a) Social Security Number trace; (b) multi-state criminal records search; (c) county criminal records search for all counties of residence within the past 7 years; (d) National Sex Offender Registry search; (e) California Department of Justice (DOJ) LiveScan fingerprint check where required by California law.

4.3 The agency shall not make any adverse employment decision based solely on a criminal history finding without first conducting an individualized assessment per the California Fair Chance Act (AB 1008) and EEOC enforcement guidance, considering: (a) the nature and gravity of the offense; (b) the time elapsed since the offense or completion of sentence; (c) the nature of the job sought and its relationship to the offense.

4.4 Convictions that are automatically disqualifying regardless of individualized assessment include: (a) any offense requiring sex offender registration; (b) any conviction for abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a minor, elderly, or dependent adult; (c) any conviction for healthcare fraud; (d) any conviction currently resulting in exclusion from federal healthcare program participation.

4.5 All current employees shall be subject to periodic rescreening every 3 years or immediately upon credible report of criminal conduct, whichever occurs first.

4.6 All background check records shall be maintained in a separate, secured confidential file — not in the general personnel file — accessible only to the HR Director, Administrator, and Compliance Officer, per CO-HP-007 and applicable privacy laws.

4.7 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

POLICY HR-TA-003

OIG/SAM Exclusion Screening

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes mandatory requirements for screening all employees, contractors, vendors, volunteers, and Governing Body members of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. against the Office of Inspector General (OIG) List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) and the System for Award Management (SAM) exclusion database. Federal law (42 USC § 1320a-7 and § 1320a-7a) prohibits any entity participating in federal healthcare programs from employing or contracting with excluded individuals. Employing an excluded individual exposes the agency to civil monetary penalties of up to $100,000 per instance, treble damages, program exclusion, and criminal prosecution. This policy ensures that the agency maintains continuous compliance through pre-employment screening, monthly ongoing screening, and immediate response protocols when an exclusion is identified.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

All current and prospective employees (full-time, part-time, per diem)

All contracted staff, staffing agency personnel, and independent contractors

All Governing Body members (per GV-GB-001 § 6.1.4)

All vendors and suppliers with direct or indirect access to patients, patient information, or federal healthcare program funds

All volunteers per HR-WM-006

The HR Director, Compliance Officer, and all personnel responsible for conducting or overseeing exclusion screening

This policy applies regardless of whether the individual has direct patient contact. Any individual who is employed by, contracted with, or providing services on behalf of a Medicare-certified home health agency must be screened.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall screen every individual identified in Section 3 against both the OIG LEIE and SAM exclusion databases prior to employment, engagement, or appointment, and monthly thereafter without exception.

4.2 No individual shall perform any work, provide any services, or conduct any business on behalf of the agency until an initial exclusion screening has been completed and documented as CLEAR.

4.3 If an individual is found to be excluded from participation in federal healthcare programs at any point — during pre-employment screening, monthly ongoing screening, or through any other means — the agency shall immediately remove the individual from all duties and initiate the response protocol defined in this policy.

4.4 The Compliance Officer shall be notified within 24 hours of any exclusion match. The Compliance Officer shall oversee the investigation and, if the exclusion is confirmed, ensure self-disclosure to the OIG per applicable self-disclosure protocols.

4.5 Monthly screening shall be conducted between the 1st and 15th of each calendar month. Results shall be documented and retained per this policy.

4.6 The agency shall screen not only by name but also by any known aliases, and shall use NPI (National Provider Identifier) where available, to reduce false negatives and false positives.

4.7 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

POLICY HR-TA-004

Licensure & Certification Verification

1. Policy Header

2. Purpose

This policy establishes mandatory requirements for verifying and continuously monitoring the professional licensure, certification, and registration status of all clinical and licensed personnel employed by or contracted with Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. Verification of licensure is required by 42 CFR § 484.105 and § 484.115 (Personnel Qualifications), California Health and Safety Code home health agency licensing requirements, and applicable professional practice acts. Employing individuals who do not hold current, valid, unrestricted licensure appropriate to their role exposes the agency to regulatory sanctions, survey deficiencies, malpractice liability, and criminal prosecution for unauthorized practice. This policy ensures that every licensed professional practicing under the agency's authority holds a valid, verified license at all times.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

All Registered Nurses (RN), Licensed Vocational Nurses (LVN), Physical Therapists (PT), Physical Therapist Assistants (PTA), Occupational Therapists (OT), Certified Occupational Therapy Assistants (COTA), Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP), Medical Social Workers (MSW), and any other licensed/certified clinical professional employed by or contracted with the agency

Home Health Aides (HHA) — certification verification per 42 CFR § 484.80

All contracted/staffing agency clinical personnel

The HR Director, Director of Nursing, and all personnel responsible for licensure verification

The Administrator

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall verify the current, valid, unrestricted status of every professional license, certification, and registration required for each clinical position prior to the individual performing any clinical duties, and shall continuously monitor licensure status throughout employment.

4.2 Verification shall be conducted through primary source verification — direct confirmation with the issuing state licensing board, certification body, or their authorized electronic verification system. Copies of licenses provided by the applicant or employee are not sufficient as the sole means of verification.

4.3 No individual shall provide clinical services, conduct patient assessments, or perform any task requiring professional licensure until primary source verification is completed and documented as CURRENT and UNRESTRICTED.

4.4 The agency shall maintain a Licensure Expiration Tracking System (Appendix A) to proactively monitor all license expiration dates and initiate renewal verification at least 60 calendar days before expiration.

4.5 Any clinician whose license expires, is suspended, revoked, or subject to restriction shall be immediately removed from clinical duties until the licensure issue is fully resolved. Practice on an expired or restricted license is a violation of California law and agency policy.

4.6 All licensure verification records shall be maintained in the employee's personnel file per HR-WM-005 and HR-WM-007.

4.7 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid.

POLICY HR-ER-004 — Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination

Header: Policy ID: HR-ER-004 | REQUIRED | Tier 3 — Confidential | Annual Review | HR Director | ACTIVE | v6.0

2. Purpose

Establishes the agency's zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, and retaliation in compliance with Title VII, ADA, ADEA, California FEHA, and SB 1343 mandatory harassment prevention training requirements. Protects all employees, applicants, patients, and third parties.

3. Scope

All employees, applicants, contractors, vendors, patients, and visitors. Covers all forms of harassment (sexual, racial, religious, age-based, disability-based, and all other protected classes).

4. Policy Statements

4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. maintains zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, and retaliation based on any protected characteristic.

4.2 All employees are required to report harassment immediately. Supervisors who become aware of potential harassment must report within 24 hours.

4.3 All complaints will be promptly investigated with appropriate confidentiality.

4.4 Substantiated harassment will result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination per HR-ER-002.

4.5 All employees shall complete 2 hours of harassment prevention training annually per California SB 1343 (supervisors: additional component). Training tracked per HR-TD-001.

POLICY HR-ER-005 — Substance Abuse & Drug-Free Workplace

Header: Policy ID: HR-ER-005 | REQUIRED | Tier 3 — Confidential | Annual Review | HR Director | ACTIVE | v6.0

2. Purpose

Establishes the agency's drug-free workplace policy in compliance with the Drug-Free Workplace Act, California law, and the agency's obligation to ensure patient safety. Defines prohibited conduct, testing protocols, consequences, and employee assistance resources.

3. Scope

All employees. Pre-employment testing per HR-TA-001.

4. Policy Statements

4.1 The manufacture, distribution, dispensation, possession, or use of illegal drugs, or reporting to work under the influence of drugs or alcohol, is prohibited.

4.2 The agency may conduct drug/alcohol testing: (a) pre-employment; (b) reasonable suspicion; (c) post-accident; (d) return-to-duty; (e) follow-up. Testing shall comply with California law regarding permissible testing and employee privacy.

4.3 A positive test result or refusal to submit to testing shall result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination.

4.4 Employees voluntarily seeking help for substance abuse before a violation is identified are encouraged to use the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) and may be eligible for a leave of absence per applicable law. Voluntary disclosure does not shield an employee from consequences if a violation has already occurred or been reported.

4.5 Prescription medication use that may impair job performance must be reported to the employee's supervisor or HR Director.
