## 2. Purpose
This policy establishes procurement standards, cost controls, and inventory management requirements for medical supplies and equipment used by Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The policy ensures that:
- Procurement follows a standardised, documented process.
- Vendor competitive bidding is performed for purchases exceeding $2,500.
- Inventory is tracked and waste is prevented.
- Equipment is maintained and replaced on a defined schedule.
- Annual supply cost analysis is performed.
- All supply and equipment expenditures are aligned with the approved annual budget under FN-FP-005.

## 3. Scope
This policy applies to:
- The CFO (final procurement authorising party for purchases above defined thresholds).
- The Administrator.
- The Director of Nursing / Clinical Manager (for clinical supply and equipment requisition).
- Any designated Procurement Coordinator or Operations Manager.
- All staff who request, receive, store, or use agency-procured supplies and equipment.

## 4. Policy Statements
4.1 All procurement of medical supplies and equipment shall follow the agency's standardised procurement process documented in this policy and shall not be initiated outside the approved process.
4.2 Any single procurement action with an expected value greater than $2,500 shall be subject to documented competitive bidding from at least three qualified vendors where available.
4.3 All supplies and equipment received shall be entered into the Inventory Tracking Log (Appendix B) at the point of receipt.
4.4 Equipment requiring scheduled maintenance shall be maintained on the documented preventive maintenance and replacement schedule.
4.5 An annual supply cost analysis shall be performed by the CFO and presented as part of the budget development cycle under FN-FP-005.
4.6 Supply and equipment expenditure shall remain aligned with the approved annual budget; any line item projected to exceed budget by more than 10% shall trigger corrective action under FN-FP-005 §4.4.
4.7 No vendor relationship shall be established with an entity appearing on the OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) or the SAM exclusion database, per HR-TA-003.
4.8 Only the most current approved version of this policy shall be considered valid; superseded versions must not be used for any procurement or financial control purpose.

## 5. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Procurement | The process by which the agency acquires medical supplies, equipment, and related goods from external vendors. |
| Competitive Bidding | The structured solicitation of price and service offers from at least three qualified vendors for a single procurement action above the threshold. |
| Inventory | The complete set of supplies and equipment owned or controlled by the agency at any point in time, recorded in the Inventory Tracking Log. |
| Preventive Maintenance | Scheduled servicing of equipment performed at defined intervals to maintain operational reliability and clinical safety. |
| Annual Supply Cost Analysis | The yearly review of supply expenditure performed by the CFO as input to the next budget cycle. |

## 6. Procedures
### 6.1 Procurement Authorisation
6.1.1 The requesting party shall complete a Procurement Authorisation Form (Appendix A) identifying the supply or equipment, quantity, expected unit cost, justification, and applicable budget line.
6.1.2 Requests below the competitive bidding threshold shall be approved by the Administrator or designee. Requests at or above $2,500 shall be approved by the CFO.

### 6.2 Competitive Bidding
6.2.1 For any single procurement action above $2,500, the Procurement Coordinator shall obtain price and service quotations from at least three qualified vendors where available.
6.2.2 Bids shall be summarised on the Procurement Authorisation Form with a documented selection rationale; selection shall be on the basis of total value (price, service, quality, and risk), not lowest price alone.
6.2.3 If three qualified vendors cannot be identified, the rationale shall be documented and the CFO shall approve the sole-source or limited-source selection.

### 6.3 Vendor Eligibility Verification
6.3.1 Prior to establishing a new vendor relationship, the Procurement Coordinator shall verify that the vendor does not appear on the OIG LEIE or SAM exclusion lists per HR-TA-003.
6.3.2 Vendor eligibility shall be re-verified at contract renewal.

### 6.4 Receiving and Inventory Tracking
6.4.1 All supplies and equipment received shall be inspected against the purchase order and entered into the Inventory Tracking Log (Appendix B) at the point of receipt.
6.4.2 Discrepancies between order and receipt shall be reported to the Procurement Coordinator within 1 business day for vendor follow-up.
6.4.3 Issuance of supplies and equipment to clinical staff shall be logged so that on-hand quantities remain accurate and waste can be detected.

### 6.5 Equipment Maintenance and Replacement
6.5.1 Equipment requiring scheduled maintenance shall be entered on the preventive maintenance schedule with assigned responsibility and due dates.
6.5.2 Equipment past its defined replacement interval, or unserviceable, shall be removed from clinical use and dispositioned per the replacement schedule.

### 6.6 Annual Supply Cost Analysis
6.6.1 The CFO shall perform an annual supply cost analysis comparing the prior year's supply expenditure to budget and to clinical volume.
6.6.2 Results shall feed into the next budget cycle under FN-FP-005.

## 7. Documentation Requirements
- Completed Procurement Authorisation Form (Appendix A) for each procurement action.
- Inventory Tracking Log (Appendix B) maintained on a continuous basis.
- Bid summaries for all procurements above the competitive bidding threshold.
- Preventive maintenance and equipment replacement schedules retained per CO-HP-007.
- Vendor exclusion screening records per HR-TA-003.
- Signed Policy Acknowledgment (Appendix C) for all personnel within scope.

## 8. Compliance & Audit
- 100% of procurement actions above $2,500 shall have documented competitive bidding (or a CFO-approved sole-source rationale).
- 100% of new vendors shall have OIG LEIE / SAM exclusion verification on file before any purchase order is issued.
- Inventory variance shall be investigated and resolved within 5 business days of identification.
- Supply expenditure variance from budget shall be reviewed monthly per FN-FP-005 §4.4.

## 10. Training Requirements
All personnel with procurement, receiving, or inventory management responsibilities shall be trained on this policy within 14 calendar days of assignment and annually thereafter. Training shall cover the procurement authorisation workflow, the competitive bidding threshold, vendor eligibility verification, and inventory tracking. Completion shall be tracked in the LMS per HR-TR-101.

## 11. Version Control
This policy is maintained under the agency's enterprise policy lifecycle management system per EN-LC-001. Only the most current approved version is valid. Substantive revision requires Governing Body approval and re-acknowledgment by all personnel within scope within 14 calendar days of the revised effective date.
