## 2. Purpose
This policy establishes criteria for classifying and managing bad debt write-offs and charity care at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The policy ensures that:
- No patient is denied medically necessary services due to inability to pay.
- Financial hardship screening is performed before any patient account is referred for collection.
- Charity care eligibility is determined consistently against published Federal Poverty Level (FPL) guidelines.
- Bad debt write-offs occur only after the agency has exhausted reasonable collection efforts per FN-BC-003 §6.6.
- All write-offs require CFO authorisation and are documented for audit and Governing Body reporting.
- Annual reporting of bad debt and charity care is provided to the Governing Body for fiscal oversight.

## 3. Scope
This policy applies to:
- The CFO (authorising party for all write-offs).
- The Revenue Cycle Director.
- Patient Account / billing personnel responsible for collections and write-off processing.
- The Compliance Officer (oversight of charity care policy application).
- The Governing Body (annual reporting recipient).
- Any contracted collection agency engaged on behalf of the agency.

## 4. Policy Statements
4.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall not deny medically necessary services to any patient on the basis of inability to pay.
4.2 Every patient account that becomes financially distressed shall undergo financial hardship screening prior to any referral for external collection.
4.3 Charity care eligibility shall be determined using the published HHS Federal Poverty Level (FPL) guidelines and the agency's Charity Care Application (Appendix A).
4.4 Bad debt write-offs shall occur only after reasonable collection efforts have been exhausted in accordance with FN-BC-003 §6.6.
4.5 No patient account shall be written off as bad debt or approved as charity care without written authorisation from the CFO recorded on the Bad Debt Write-Off Authorisation form (Appendix B).
4.6 Bad debt and charity care totals shall be reported to the Governing Body at least annually, with quarterly trending reviewed by the CFO and Administrator.
4.7 Patient accounts being actively considered for charity care shall not be referred to collection during the eligibility determination period.
4.8 Only the most current approved version of this policy and the current FPL thresholds shall be considered valid; superseded versions must not be used for eligibility determination.

## 5. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Bad Debt | Amounts owed by patients which the agency has determined to be uncollectible after reasonable collection effort, written off in accordance with this policy and FN-BC-003. |
| Charity Care | Health care services provided without expectation of payment to patients meeting the agency's published financial hardship eligibility criteria. |
| Financial Hardship Screening | The structured assessment of a patient's household income against published FPL guidelines used to determine eligibility for charity care or other financial assistance. |
| FPL | Federal Poverty Level — the income thresholds published annually by HHS used as the baseline for charity care eligibility determination. |
| Reasonable Collection Effort | The documented sequence of patient billing notices, contact attempts, and external collection activity required by FN-BC-003 §6.6 before an account may be written off as bad debt. |

## 6. Procedures
### 6.1 Financial Hardship Screening
6.1.1 When a patient indicates inability to pay, or when an account becomes 90 days past due without payment, the Patient Account representative shall offer financial hardship screening using the Charity Care Application (Appendix A).
6.1.2 The patient or responsible party shall provide household income documentation as requested on the application.
6.1.3 The Revenue Cycle Director shall compare reported household income against the current published FPL thresholds and assign the appropriate eligibility category.

### 6.2 Charity Care Determination
6.2.1 Completed Charity Care Applications shall be reviewed by the Revenue Cycle Director and submitted to the CFO with a recommendation.
6.2.2 The CFO shall approve, modify, or deny each charity care request and document the determination on the application.
6.2.3 Approved charity care shall be applied to the account and the patient notified in writing.

### 6.3 Bad Debt Write-Off Authorisation
6.3.1 Accounts that have completed reasonable collection effort per FN-BC-003 §6.6 without recovery shall be presented to the CFO for write-off review on the Bad Debt Write-Off Authorisation form (Appendix B).
6.3.2 The CFO shall authorise or deny each write-off in writing; no write-off shall be processed without authorisation.
6.3.3 Authorised write-offs shall be posted to the patient account with the authorisation reference retained in the audit trail.

### 6.4 Reporting
6.4.1 The CFO shall track bad debt and charity care totals quarterly and present trended results to the Administrator.
6.4.2 An annual bad debt and charity care report shall be presented to the Governing Body for review consistent with the Governing Body's financial oversight role under GV-GB-001 §6.2.5.

## 7. Documentation Requirements
- Completed Charity Care Application (Appendix A) for each financial hardship screening.
- Signed Bad Debt Write-Off Authorisation (Appendix B) for every account written off.
- Quarterly bad debt and charity care trend reports retained per CO-HP-007.
- Annual Governing Body report retained in the governance file per GV-GB-001.
- Signed Policy Acknowledgment (Appendix C) for all personnel within scope.

## 8. Compliance & Audit
- 100% of bad debt write-offs shall have CFO authorisation on file.
- 100% of charity care determinations shall have a completed Charity Care Application on file with FPL comparison.
- No patient shall be denied medically necessary services on the basis of inability to pay.
- Annual bad debt and charity care report shall be presented to the Governing Body each fiscal year.

## 10. Training Requirements
All Revenue Cycle and Patient Account personnel shall be trained on this policy within 14 calendar days of hire or assignment, and annually thereafter. Training shall cover the financial hardship screening process, charity care eligibility determination using current FPL thresholds, and the bad debt write-off authorisation process. Completion shall be tracked in the LMS per HR-TR-101.
