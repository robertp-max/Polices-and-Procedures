## 2. Purpose
This policy establishes standards for accurate charge capture for all services delivered by Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. and for the maintenance of the agency fee schedule. The policy ensures that:
- All billable services are captured with correct charge codes, amounts, and dates of service.
- The agency fee schedule is reviewed and updated annually in alignment with CMS rate changes.
- Charges are reconciled against services delivered through the visit log and clinical documentation.
- Charge capture errors are identified, investigated, and corrected on a routine basis.

## 3. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All billing coordinators and billing specialists.
- The Revenue Cycle Director.
- The CFO.
- Clinical scheduling coordinators responsible for visit logging.
- Any contracted billing service performing charge capture functions on behalf of the agency.

## 4. Policy Statements
4.1 All billable services rendered by the agency shall be captured in the billing system with the correct charge code, charge amount, and date of service.
4.2 The agency fee schedule shall be reviewed and updated at least annually, no later than January 1 of each calendar year, to reflect current CMS PDGM payment rates and any payer-specific rate changes governed under FN-FP-001.
4.3 Charge capture shall be reconciled against the visit log and clinical documentation for every 30-day payment period prior to final claim release per FN-BC-001.
4.4 Any charge capture error identified shall be corrected through the documented charge correction process and tracked to closure by the Revenue Cycle Director.
4.5 A formal charge capture audit shall be performed quarterly by the Revenue Cycle Director with results reported to the CFO.
4.6 Charge accuracy shall meet or exceed 99% for any rolling quarterly review period; any deviation triggers root cause analysis per QA-AE-002.
4.7 Only the most current approved version of this policy and the current fee schedule shall be considered valid; superseded versions must not be used for billing or compliance purposes.

## 5. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Charge Capture | The process of recording all billable services delivered to a patient with the correct charge code, charge amount, and date of service for inclusion on a claim. |
| Fee Schedule | The agency's master list of charge codes and corresponding charge amounts for all billable services. |
| Charge Capture Reconciliation | The structured comparison of charges recorded against the visit log and clinical documentation to confirm completeness and accuracy. |
| Charge Correction | The documented process of adjusting a charge previously recorded in error, including reason, authorising party, and audit trail. |

## 6. Procedures
### 6.1 Charge Capture Workflow
6.1.1 At the time each visit is completed and documentation is finalised, the assigned clinician shall close the visit in the EHR which generates the billable charge for that encounter.
6.1.2 The Billing Coordinator shall verify that every visit logged for the 30-day payment period has a corresponding charge captured in the billing system before the period is closed.
6.1.3 Any visit completed without a corresponding charge, or any charge captured without a corresponding documented visit, shall be flagged on the Charge Capture Reconciliation Log (Appendix B) for investigation within 5 business days.

### 6.2 Annual Fee Schedule Update
6.2.1 The Revenue Cycle Director shall obtain the CMS PDGM final rule rate update each year and apply the corresponding charge amount changes to the agency fee schedule no later than January 1.
6.2.2 Payer-specific rates derived from contracts maintained under FN-FP-001 shall be reviewed at the same time and updated where applicable.
6.2.3 The CFO shall approve the updated fee schedule before it is activated in the billing system.

### 6.3 Charge Reconciliation Against Visit Log
6.3.1 Prior to final claim release per FN-BC-001 §6.1, the Billing Coordinator shall reconcile charges to the visit log and clinical documentation for the 30-day payment period.
6.3.2 Discrepancies shall be resolved before claim release; unresolved discrepancies shall trigger a billing hold per FN-BC-001 §4.5.

### 6.4 Charge Correction Process
6.4.1 Identified charge errors shall be corrected only through the documented charge correction process with a recorded reason, authorising party, and date.
6.4.2 Corrections shall be retained in the billing system audit trail and shall not overwrite the original record.

### 6.5 Quarterly Charge Capture Audit
6.5.1 The Revenue Cycle Director shall perform a quarterly charge capture audit on a representative sample drawn from each clinical discipline.
6.5.2 Audit results, error rate, and corrective actions shall be reported to the CFO and integrated into the QAPI program per QA-PI-002.

## 7. Documentation Requirements
- Current agency fee schedule (Appendix A) with version date and CFO approval signature.
- Charge Capture Reconciliation Log (Appendix B) for each 30-day payment period.
- Quarterly charge capture audit reports retained for the period required by CO-HP-007.
- Signed Policy Acknowledgment (Appendix C) for all personnel within scope.

## 8. Compliance & Audit
- 100% of services delivered shall have a corresponding captured charge.
- Charge accuracy shall be ≥ 99% on any quarterly audit.
- Fee schedule shall be updated and CFO-approved annually no later than January 1.
- Charge capture and reconciliation logs shall be available for survey, payer audit, and internal compliance review at all times.

## 10. Training Requirements
All billing coordinators and clinical scheduling coordinators shall be trained on this policy within 14 calendar days of hire or assignment, and annually thereafter. Training shall cover the charge capture workflow, the reconciliation process, the charge correction process, and the quarterly audit cycle. Completion shall be tracked in the LMS per HR-TR-101.
