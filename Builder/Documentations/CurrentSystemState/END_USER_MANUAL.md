# End User Manual

Audience: non-technical operational users.

## Dashboard

- Purpose:
  - quick view of current compliance posture, upcoming obligations, and status indicators.
- How to use:
  - open `Dashboard`, review upcoming and at-risk items, open item details.
- What not to do:
  - do not treat dashboard summaries as final evidence; open event/workflow details before certifying actions.

## Policy Library

- Purpose:
  - browse policy catalog, open policy details, print approved policy content.
- How to use:
  - go to `Library`, search/filter by policy ID or domain, open details, use print when needed.
- What not to do:
  - do not rely on old external copies of policy docs when updated in app.

## Forms Library

- Purpose:
  - locate required forms and complete supporting documentation.
- How to use:
  - open `Forms`, select form, fill fields, save/sign as directed.
- What not to do:
  - do not submit forms without verifying the event/workflow context.

## Workflows

- Purpose:
  - execute step-by-step operational workflows tied to regulatory requirements.
- How to use:
  - open `Workflows`, select workflow, complete steps/forms/approvals in order.
- What not to do:
  - do not skip required steps or mark complete before supporting evidence is attached.

## Compliance Calendar

- Purpose:
  - view required events and deadlines by date.
- How to use:
  - open `Calendar`, select event, follow event stage workflow.
- What not to do:
  - do not assume completed calendar appearance means certified closure.

## Evidence Center

- Purpose:
  - manage evidence files linked to policy/workflow/event context.
- How to use:
  - select event, upload evidence, verify status and audit entries.
- What not to do:
  - do not upload files without correct event/policy/workflow mapping.

## Audit Mode

- Purpose:
  - review readiness before certification and survey export.
- How to use:
  - open `Audit`, review missing items, resolve blockers, export packets.
- What not to do:
  - do not certify while checklist items are failing.

## Brad / iAdministrator

- Purpose:
  - ask system questions, review references, and understand compliance context.
- How to use:
  - ask direct operational/compliance questions and review cited references.
- What not to do:
  - do not treat uncited statements as final; confirm references.

## Help Center

- Purpose:
  - find guided support content by module and task.
- How to use:
  - open `Help`, pick module article, follow steps.
- What not to do:
  - do not use outdated screenshots from external docs if app behavior differs.

## Guided Tour

- Purpose:
  - onboarding walkthrough for first-time users.
- How to use:
  - follow prompts, complete core flow once, restart when retraining.
- What not to do:
  - do not skip sections if assigned onboarding completion requirements.

## Search

- Purpose:
  - quickly locate policies/forms/events/evidence IDs.
- How to use:
  - use module search fields and ID filters (policy/form/event/evidence IDs).
- What not to do:
  - do not rely on partial ID guesswork for compliance-critical uploads.

## User Profile / Account

- Purpose:
  - account access, password/session management.
- How to use:
  - sign in, keep credentials secure, refresh session when prompted.
- What not to do:
  - do not share credentials or actor identity fields used in demo mode.

---

## End-User Safety Checklist

- always bind actions to the correct event;
- complete required forms before certification;
- attach evidence to the right form/task when possible;
- verify audit/checklist state before closure;
- use Help Center when uncertain rather than bypassing workflow controls.
