## 2. Purpose

This policy establishes the enterprise-wide taxonomy, classification standards, and metadata schema governing how all policies, procedures, and related governance documents are structured, identified, categorized, and maintained at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. The taxonomy is the backbone of the agency's policy governance framework and enables:

- Unique, durable identification of every policy across all regulatory jurisdictions and operational domains
- Classification of policies by domain, subdomain, tier, lifecycle status, and access protection level
- Consistent metadata that supports audit trail, survey readiness, and regulatory traceability
- Cross-reference integrity across the 10-domain policy library
- Compliant management of the policy library per OIG Corporate Compliance Program Element 1 (written standards) and CMS 42 CFR §484.105 (administrative organization)

This policy governs the taxonomic rules. Lifecycle procedures (approval, review cycles, version control, archival) are governed by EN-LC-001.

---

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

* All policy documents issued under the authority of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. across all 10 governance domains (GV, CL, QA, HR, CO, FN, OP, IT, RM, EN)
* All staff responsible for drafting, reviewing, approving, or publishing policy documents
* The Compliance Officer, Administrator, and all Domain Stewards responsible for domain-level policy governance
* All policy management systems (electronic and physical) used to store, retrieve, and publish policies

This policy does not govern clinical protocols, operational checklists, or job aids that are sub-documents issued under a parent policy. Those items are classified as "controlled documents" under EN-LC-001 but use a simplified form of the taxonomy.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| Taxonomy | The structured classification system by which all policies are organized into hierarchical domains, subdomains, and sequential policy numbers, with standardized metadata. |
| Domain | A primary organizational unit of the policy library representing a major functional area. The agency currently has 10 domains: GV, CL, QA, HR, CO, FN, OP, IT, RM, EN. |
| Subdomain | A secondary classification within a domain representing a specific program, functional area, or regulatory cluster within the domain. Each subdomain has a 2-letter code unique within its domain. |
| Policy ID | The unique identifier for each policy, constructed as: [Domain Code]-[Subdomain Code]-[Sequence Number] (e.g., GV-GB-001). Policy IDs are permanent; a policy ID retired upon policy sunset is never reused. |
| Tier | The classification of a policy's regulatory/operational urgency: REQUIRED (directly mandated by federal or state regulation, licensure, or CMS CoP), ESSENTIAL (industry standard or accreditation required), RECOMMENDED (best practice), GOOD TO HAVE (operational enhancement). |
| Lifecycle Status | The stage of a policy's active lifecycle: Draft, Under Review, Active (approved and in force), Sunset Pending, Archived. |
| Access Tier | The information sensitivity classification for the policy document itself: Tier 1 — Public, Tier 2 — Restricted (agency staff only), Tier 3 — Confidential (role-limited access), Tier 4 — Privileged (Governing Body / Executive only). |
| Domain Steward | The designated role responsible for the content integrity of all policies within a domain. Domain Stewards are the subject matter authorities for their domain. |
| Metadata | The structured data elements required for each policy record: Policy ID, Title, Domain, Subdomain, Tier, Lifecycle Status, Review Cycle, Access Tier, Owner/Steward, Effective Date, Version, Regulatory References, Cross-References. |

## 5. Policy Statement

5.1 All policy documents issued by Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall conform to the taxonomy and classification standards established in this policy. Non-conforming documents shall not be considered official agency policies.

5.2 Every policy shall be assigned a permanent, unique Policy ID upon creation (per Section 6.1). Policy IDs shall not be modified after assignment. A policy that is significantly revised retains its original ID with an incremented version number.

5.3 Every policy shall contain all required metadata fields as specified in Section 6.2. Policies that are missing required metadata fields shall be returned to the drafting owner for completion before approval.

5.4 The Tier classification (REQUIRED, ESSENTIAL, RECOMMENDED, GOOD TO HAVE) shall reflect the regulatory basis of each policy as determined by the Compliance Officer at the time of classification. Tier classification changes require Compliance Officer approval.

5.5 The 10-domain structure is the authoritative organizational framework for this agency's policy library. New domains may only be added by action of the Administrator with Governing Body notification. New subdomains may be added by the Compliance Officer with Administrator approval. Both require updates to this taxonomy policy within 90 days.

5.6 All cross-references embedded in any policy must use the standard Policy ID of the referenced policy. Free-text titles without Policy IDs are not acceptable in cross-reference sections.

5.7 The Compliance Officer shall maintain a current Policy Registry (Appendix A) — a master index of all policies with their taxonomy metadata — and shall update the Registry within 7 calendar days of any policy action (new policy, revision, sunset, or archive).

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 Policy ID Assignment

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.1.1 | Policy Drafter / Domain Steward | Before drafting a new policy, submit a Policy ID Request Form (Appendix B) to the Compliance Officer specifying: (a) proposed domain; (b) proposed subdomain (existing or new); (c) brief description of the policy scope; (d) intended Tier classification. | Before commencing policy draft. |
| 6.1.2 | Compliance Officer | Review the Policy ID Request Form: (a) confirm the proposed domain and subdomain are consistent with the taxonomy; (b) verify no existing policy already covers the proposed scope (duplication check against the Policy Registry); (c) if a new subdomain is required, create the subdomain record in the Registry and assign the next available 3-digit sequence number within that subdomain; (d) assign and return the Policy ID in writing. | Within 5 business days of receiving the request form. |
| 6.1.3 | Compliance Officer | Record the new Policy ID assignment in the Policy Registry (Appendix A) with status "Draft" and the date of assignment, before the policy document is complete. | At assignment. |
| 6.1.4 | Compliance Officer | Reserve retired Policy IDs permanently in the Registry with status "Archived" or "Sunset." Do not reassign any retired Policy ID to a new policy. | Continuous. |

### 6.2 Required Metadata Standards

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.2.1 | Policy Drafter | Every policy document submitted for review shall include all of the following metadata fields in the Policy Header table (Section 1): Policy ID; Policy Title; Domain; Subdomain; Classification Tier; Status; Review Cycle; Access Tier; Policy Owner/Steward; Effective Date; Version; Approved By; Last Reviewed; Next Review Date; Supersedes. Fields may not be left blank — use "N/A" where a field is genuinely not applicable. | At initial draft submission. |
| 6.2.2 | Compliance Officer | During policy review, verify all metadata fields are complete and accurate before the policy advances to approval. Reject for correction if any required field is blank or non-conforming. | During pre-approval review. |
| 6.2.3 | Domain Steward | Ensure the Regulatory References and Cross-References sections of every policy use: (a) for regulatory references: the specific CFR, CCR, or statute citation (e.g., "42 CFR §484.55"); (b) for cross-references: the standard Policy ID format (e.g., "CL-CA-001"). Free-text descriptions without legal citations or Policy IDs are not acceptable. | At time of drafting; verified at review. |

### 6.3 Tier Classification Standards

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.3.1 | Compliance Officer | Classify each policy's Tier at the time of ID assignment using the following standards: (a) REQUIRED — the policy addresses a requirement that is directly mandated by a federal statute, federal regulation (CFR), CMS Condition of Participation, California state statute, California regulation (CCR), licensure requirement, or Court order; non-compliance could result in citation, license suspension, or Medicare certification termination; (b) ESSENTIAL — the policy addresses an accreditation standard (e.g., ACHC, CHAP), OIG enforcement guidance, or an industry standard where non-compliance represents a significant organizational risk; (c) RECOMMENDED — industry best practice where adoption is common and expected but not directly enforceable by a regulatory body; (d) GOOD TO HAVE — operational or efficiency enhancement with no direct regulatory basis. | At ID assignment; verified at annual review. |
| 6.3.2 | Compliance Officer | Maintain the Tier Basis Log (Appendix C) for all REQUIRED-tier policies documenting the specific regulatory citation that mandates the policy. The Tier Basis Log shall be reviewed annually and updated within 30 days of any regulatory change that affects a policy's Tier classification. | Maintained continuously; annual review. |
| 6.3.3 | Domain Steward | If a policy's Tier classification changes as a result of a regulatory change, notify the Compliance Officer in writing within 30 days of identifying the change. The Compliance Officer shall update the Policy Registry and the policy's metadata within 7 business days of receiving the notification. | Within 30 days of identifying change; Registry update within 7 days. |

### 6.4 Domain and Subdomain Management

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.4.1 | Compliance Officer | Maintain the official Domain Registry (Appendix D) specifying for each domain: Domain Code, Domain Name, Domain Steward role, and brief description. The Domain Registry is updated only when a domain is added or modified per the governance requirements in Section 5.5. | Maintained continuously. |
| 6.4.2 | Compliance Officer | Maintain the official Subdomain Registry (Appendix E) specifying for each subdomain: Subdomain ID (e.g., GV-GB), Subdomain Code, Subdomain Name, Domain, Steward, Review Cycle, Access Tier, and description. | Maintained continuously; updated within 30 days of any subdomain addition or modification. |
| 6.4.3 | Domain Stewards | Review the subdomain structure and any subdomain assignments for their domain at least annually to ensure the taxonomic structure remains current and accurately reflects the agency's regulatory landscape. Report gaps to the Compliance Officer. | Annually; report within annual policy review cycle. |

### 6.5 Policy Registry Maintenance

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.5.1 | Compliance Officer | Maintain the Policy Registry (Appendix A) as a live, always-current master index of all policies including: Policy ID, Title, Domain, Subdomain, Tier, Lifecycle Status, Current Version, Review Cycle, Last Review Date, Next Review Date, and Regulatory Basis (for REQUIRED-tier policies). The Registry shall be accessible to the Administrator and all Domain Stewards at all times. | Maintained continuously; updated within 7 calendar days of any policy action. |
| 6.5.2 | Compliance Officer | Produce a Policy Registry Summary Report for the Administrator monthly. The report shall flag: (a) any policy overdue for review; (b) any REQUIRED-tier policy in "Draft" status beyond 90 days; (c) any policy lacking a designated Domain Steward. | Monthly. |
| 6.5.3 | Compliance Officer | Present a Policy Registry Audit Report to the Governing Body annually as part of the compliance report per CO-CP-003. The annual report shall include: total policy count by domain, count by Tier, count by Lifecycle Status, overdue review count, and any policy sunset actions taken in the prior year. | Annually at second quarterly Governing Body meeting. |

### 6.6 Escalation and Exception Handling

| Condition | Escalation Path | Corrective Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Duplicate policy discovered (two policies with overlapping scope) | Domain Steward notifies Compliance Officer. | Compliance Officer initiates consolidation review: determine which policy is primary, and whether the secondary should be sunset or merged. Complete consolidation within 90 days. | Within 90 days of identification. |
| Policy without a valid Policy ID is circulated to staff | Domain Steward or supervisor notifies Compliance Officer. | Document is removed from staff circulation until formally registered. Compliance Officer assigns ID and initiates formal review per GV-PM-001. | Remove from circulation within 2 business days. |
| Regulatory change requires new subdomain | Compliance Officer identifies change per CO-RA-001. | Create subdomain, assign ID, initiate new policy development per GV-PM-001. Update taxonomy policy within 90 days. | Subdomain created within 30 days; taxonomy policy update within 90 days. |

## 8. Compliance & Audit Considerations

### 8.1 How Compliance Is Measured

| Compliance Indicator | Measurement Method | Acceptable Standard |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Policy Registry is current (updated within 7 days of policy actions) | Spot audit comparing Registry update date vs. policy action dates | 100% of policy actions reflected within 7 calendar days |
| All REQUIRED-tier policies have Tier Basis Log entry | Review of Tier Basis Log vs. REQUIRED-tier policy count | 100% have documented regulatory citation |
| No duplicate policies (scope overlap) | Annual duplex audit during taxonomy review | 0 unresolved duplicates |
| All policies in circulation have valid Policy IDs | Random audit of documents in staff circulation | 100% of circulating documents have registered Policy IDs |
| Policy Registry Audit Report presented to Governing Body annually | Review of Governing Body minutes | Annual report documented in second quarterly minutes |

### 8.2 What Surveyors and Auditors Will Look For

CMS surveyors and OIG reviewers will specifically verify:

1. That the agency can produce a complete inventory of its policies (equivalent to the Policy Registry) demonstrating that it knows what policies it has and their status.
2. That policies referenced in a survey finding are identifiable by unique identifier and version, allowing precise determination of what was in effect at the time of a care episode.
3. That cross-references between policies are consistent and use specific policy identifiers — not vague references like "per our privacy policy."
4. That the Governing Body receives reports on the state of the policy library, confirming oversight per GV-GB-001.
5. That REQUIRED-tier policies have a documented regulatory basis — surveyors may ask "what requires you to have this policy?"

### 8.3 Common Failure Points

| Failure Point | Risk | Mitigation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| No policy inventory — agency cannot produce a list of its policies | OIG compliance vulnerability; surveyor adverse inference | Policy Registry (Appendix A) is the required master inventory; Compliance Officer owns and updates it |
| Policy IDs are inconsistent or missing | Cross-referencing fails; audit confusion | No policy issued without a registered ID; non-conforming documents removed from circulation |
| REQUIRED-tier policies not identified as such | Agency cannot prioritize review cycle; survey prep fails | Tier Basis Log (Appendix C) formally classifies all REQUIRED policies |
| Policy taxonomy not updated after new subdomain added | New policies placed in wrong domain or ad hoc | Taxonomy policy updated within 90 days of new subdomain; Compliance Officer owns update |
| Governance Body never sees policy library status report | No executive oversight of policy state | Annual Registry Audit Report required at second quarterly Governing Body meeting |
