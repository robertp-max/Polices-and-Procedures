# Do Not Touch Guardrails

These guardrails are mandatory for future implementation and agent work.

1. Do not treat `Builder/` as runtime architecture.
2. Do not hand-edit generated files unless the documented generator flow explicitly requires it.
3. Do not remove policy/workflow/form IDs from runtime linking paths.
4. Do not break print routes or remove print pages without verified replacements.
5. Do not weaken evidence chain-of-custody requirements.
6. Do not bypass policy/workflow/event triplet validation in evidence flows.
7. Do not delete or silently rewrite audit history.
8. Do not make locked/certified evidence editable.
9. Do not remove Brad citation/guardrail logic.
10. Do not introduce PHI into demo/test data.
11. Do not introduce pastel/3D style shifts that conflict with current design system direction.
12. Do not split task identity across views into incompatible ID systems.
13. Do not make PM boards use separate unsynced task sources.
14. Do not remove current app routes unless redirect replacements are provided and tested.

## Additional execution rules

- Always label findings as implemented/partial/stub/planned/not-found.
- Mark uncertain findings as `Needs confirmation`.
- Keep evidence model and status vocabulary canonical and explicit across modules.
