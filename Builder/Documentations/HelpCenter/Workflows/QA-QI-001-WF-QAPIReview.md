# Workflow: QAPI Review (QA-QI-001-WF)

**Workflow ID:** `QA-QI-001-WF`  
**Domain:** Quality Assurance (QA)  
**Linked Policy:** `QA-QI-001` — Quality Assessment and Performance Improvement Policy  
**Risk Band:** `high`  
**Cadence Kind:** `time_based`  
**Cadence Interval:** `monthly` (quarterly for formal review)

---

## Trigger

This workflow is triggered **monthly** for data collection and **quarterly** for the formal QAPI committee review. The `event_id` format is:
- Monthly: `qapi_data_collection-{YYYYMM}-01`
- Quarterly: `qapi_review-{YYYYMMDD}-01`

Quarterly events are generated by `autogenStore` on the first day of each quarter.

---

## Steps

### Monthly Data Collection Steps
| # | Step | Role | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect quality indicator data (falls, infections, re-hospitalizations, complaints) | QA Coordinator | Yes |
| 2 | Complete the monthly data collection form | QA Coordinator | Yes |
| 3 | Submit data to QA Officer for review | QA Coordinator | Yes |

### Quarterly Review Steps
| # | Step | Role | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Prepare quarterly QAPI report with trend analysis | QA Officer | Yes |
| 5 | Identify performance improvement priorities | QA Committee | Yes |
| 6 | Develop or update Performance Improvement Projects (PIPs) | QA Committee | Yes |
| 7 | Conduct QAPI committee meeting | QA Committee Chair | Yes |
| 8 | Document meeting minutes with decisions | Secretary/Admin | Yes |
| 9 | Upload monthly data summaries as evidence | QA Coordinator | Yes |
| 10 | Upload QAPI committee meeting minutes as evidence | Administrator | Yes |
| 11 | Submit for clinical manager approval | QA Officer | Yes |
| 12 | Clinical manager approves and administrator certifies | Clinical Manager + Admin | Yes |

---

## Dependencies

- Prior month's data collection must be submitted before quarterly review begins
- `QA-QI-001` policy must be `PUBLISHED`
- QAPI committee roster must be current

## Timeline & SLA

| Milestone | Timing |
|---|---|
| Monthly data due | By the 10th of each month |
| Quarterly report due | By the 20th of the third month in each quarter |
| Certification deadline | Within 45 days of quarter end |
| SLA warning | 7 days before deadline |

---

## Exception Handling

| Exception | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Insufficient data for analysis | Document the gap and explain cause in the QAPI report |
| No quorum for committee meeting | Reschedule within 2 weeks; document in minutes |
| PIP shows no improvement | Escalate to governing body; document escalation |
| New adverse event during review period | Incorporate into current review cycle |

---

## Quality Indicators

- 100% of monthly data collections submitted on time
- Zero quarters without a completed QAPI report
- All identified improvement priorities have active PIPs
- QAPI program presented to governing body at least annually
