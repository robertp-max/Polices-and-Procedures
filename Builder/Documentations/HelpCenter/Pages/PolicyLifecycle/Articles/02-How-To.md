# Policy Lifecycle — How-To Guide

**Article:** 02-How-To  
**Page:** Policy Lifecycle (`/policy-lifecycle`)

---

## Submitting a Policy for Review

1. Navigate to `/policy-lifecycle`
2. Select the policy using the policy selector
3. Confirm the policy is in `DRAFT` state
4. Review the content — make any final edits while still in DRAFT
5. Click **Submit for Review**
6. Assign reviewer(s) if prompted
7. Click **Confirm** — the policy moves to `REVIEW` state
8. Assigned reviewers receive a notification

---

## Reviewing a Policy (Reviewer Role)

1. Open the notification or navigate to `/policy-lifecycle`
2. Select the policy under review
3. Read the full policy text
4. Use the **Review Comments Panel** to add comments:
   - Click **Add Comment** next to the section you are commenting on
   - Write your comment and click **Submit**
5. When you have reviewed all sections:
   - If the policy is ready: No action needed — your review is recorded
   - If changes are needed: Add comments and set them as "blocking"
6. The policy author will address your comments

---

## Approving or Rejecting a Policy (Admin Role)

1. Open the policy in `REVIEW` state
2. Verify all reviewer comments are resolved
3. Review the final policy text
4. To approve: Click **Approve** — policy moves to `APPROVED`
5. To reject: Click **Reject**, enter a reason — policy returns to `DRAFT`
6. To request revisions: Click **Request Revisions** — policy returns to `DRAFT` with comments

---

## Publishing a Policy (Admin Role)

1. Open the policy in `APPROVED` state
2. Confirm the effective date
3. Select publication targets if prompted (Intranet, LMS, PDF)
4. Click **Publish**
5. The policy moves to `PUBLISHED` state and becomes visible to all staff in the Policy Library
6. Staff attestation notifications are sent if configured

---

## Archiving a Policy (Super Admin Only)

1. Ensure a replacement policy has been published first
2. Open the policy in `PUBLISHED` state
3. Click **Archive**
4. Enter an archival reason and the replacing policy ID
5. Click **Confirm Archive**
6. The policy moves to `ARCHIVED` and is removed from the active library but retained in history
