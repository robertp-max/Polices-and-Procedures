# Policy Library — How-To Guide

**Article:** 02-How-To  
**Page:** Policy Library (`/library`)

---

## Finding a Policy

### By domain
1. Navigate to `/library`
2. Use the **Domain Filter** on the left or top of the page
3. Select your domain (e.g., "Clinical" or "Governance")
4. The policy grid will update to show only policies in that domain

### By keyword or ID
1. Click the **Search Bar** at the top of the page
2. Type the policy name, policy ID, or a keyword
3. Results will filter in real time
4. Example: Type "medication" to find all medication-related policies

### By status
1. Use the **Status Filter** to show only:
   - **Published** — currently active policies
   - **Draft** — policies being written (managers and admins only)
   - **Under Review** — policies pending approval
   - **Archived** — retired policies (admins/auditors only)

---

## Reading a Policy

1. Click any policy card in the grid
2. The policy detail panel opens on the right (or as a full page at `/library/:policyId`)
3. Scroll through the full policy text
4. Use the **Appendices Panel** to jump to specific sections

---

## Viewing Policy History

1. Open a policy
2. Click the **Version Selector** dropdown in the policy header
3. Select a prior version to view its content (read-only)
4. The current version is always selected by default

---

## Printing or Downloading a Policy

1. Open the policy
2. Click **Print** to open the browser print dialog with a formatted layout
3. Click **Download PDF** to generate and download a PDF version

---

## Checking if a Policy Is Current

The policy header shows:
- **Version number** (e.g., v2.1)
- **Effective date** (when this version was published)
- **Next review date** (when this policy is due for review)

If the status badge shows anything other than **Published**, contact your compliance officer before relying on that policy for operational guidance.
