# Dashboard — Overview

**Article:** 01-Overview  
**Page:** Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

---

## What This Page Does

The Dashboard is the primary command center for compliance oversight. It presents a real-time summary of where the organization stands on its regulatory obligations — what is on time, what is at risk, and what is overdue.

---

## Why It Exists

Home health agencies like Care Indeed operate under continuous regulatory oversight from CMS, state health departments, and accreditation bodies. Surveyors can arrive with little or no advance notice. The Dashboard ensures that compliance coordinators and administrators can instantly answer the question: *"Are we ready for a survey right now?"*

---

## Where It Fits in the System

The Dashboard is the **entry point** for daily compliance work. Every other part of the system — Calendar, Evidence Center, Policy Library, Compliance Sprints — feeds into the metrics shown on this page. The Dashboard does not store data; it reads from all other stores and presents an aggregated view.

```
Calendar Events → Dashboard KPIs
Evidence Store → Evidence Gap Count
Task System → My Tasks Panel
Enforcement Log → Recent Activity Feed
```

---

## Key Metrics Explained

| Metric | Meaning | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| Overdue Events | Events past their due date with no certification | Immediate attention |
| SLA Warning | Events within 7 days of deadline | Plan to complete soon |
| Evidence Gaps | Events with no accepted evidence uploaded | Upload evidence |
| Certified This Quarter | Count of events fully certified | Informational |
| Blocked Events | Events that cannot proceed due to a dependency | Resolve blockers |

---

## Who Uses This Page Daily

- **Compliance Coordinators:** Check daily for new overdue items and task assignments
- **Clinical Managers:** Review events in their domain
- **Administrators:** Monitor overall compliance health
- **Executive Sponsors:** Review certification rate trends
