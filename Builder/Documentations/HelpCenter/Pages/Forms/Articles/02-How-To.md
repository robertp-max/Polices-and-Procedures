# Forms — How-To Guide

**Article:** 02-How-To  
**Page:** Forms (`/forms`)

---

## Finding a Form

1. Navigate to `/forms`
2. Use the **Search Bar** to search by form name (e.g., "supervisory visit") or form ID (e.g., "EN-FM-002")
3. Or use the **Domain Filter** to browse forms in a specific area
4. Click the form card to open it

---

## Completing and Signing a Form

### Step 1: Open the form
Navigate to the form via `/forms` or through a workflow step that links directly to the form.

### Step 2: Read the entire document
The form content is displayed in read-only format. You must review the full content before signing.

> **Legal notice:** Signing this form constitutes a legally binding electronic acknowledgment of the document contents.

### Step 3: Complete any required fields
Some forms have input fields (dates, names, checkboxes) that must be completed before signing.

### Step 4: Sign
1. In the Signature area at the bottom, either:
   - Draw your signature on the canvas with your mouse or finger (touchscreen)
   - Type your full legal name in the text field
2. Confirm the date and your role
3. Click **Sign and Submit**

### Step 5: Confirmation
After signing:
- A confirmation message appears
- Your signature is recorded with a timestamp
- If another signer is required, they will receive a notification
- If this was the final signature, the form instance is complete

---

## Printing a Blank Form

1. Open the form
2. Click **Print Blank Form** in the action bar
3. The browser print dialog will open with a formatted blank version

---

## Viewing Your Prior Signatures

1. Open any form
2. Scroll to the **Signature History** section at the bottom
3. All prior signature events for this form (instances you were involved in) are shown with dates and roles

---

## What to Do If You Signed By Mistake

Contact your system administrator immediately. The administrator can void the form instance. The void is logged, but the original signature remains in the record as voided.
