## 1. Logging In

1. Open a browser and navigate to the application URL
2. Enter your registered email address and password
3. Click **Log In**
4. If this is your first login, you will be directed to complete account setup via the emailed setup link

**If you forgot your password:**
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**
3. Check your email and follow the link to set a new password
4. Reset links expire in 60 minutes

**If the setup email expired:** Contact your administrator to resend the setup link.

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home page after login. It shows:

- **Calendar Events Summary** — upcoming events with SLA countdowns
- **Overdue and At-Risk Events** — events requiring immediate attention (red/orange)
- **Quick Stats** — total open tasks, pending approvals, evidence gaps
- **Navigation Sidebar** — links to all major sections

**Color coding used throughout:**
| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Overdue — requires immediate action |
| Orange | SLA Warning (7 days) or SLA Urgent (3 days) |
| Green | On track / Certified |
| Blue | In progress |
| Gray | Not yet started |

## 3. Using the Master Calendar

The Master Calendar shows all compliance events scheduled for the current year.

**Viewing events:**
1. Navigate to **Calendar** in the sidebar
2. Events appear as cards organized by month
3. Click any event card to open its Event Workspace

**Filtering events:**
- Use the **Domain** filter to show only events from a specific compliance area (Clinical, HR, Finance, etc.)
- Use the **Status** filter to show only Overdue, In Progress, etc.
- Toggle between **Calendar view** and **List view** using the view controls

**Understanding event types:**
| Event Type | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Governing Body Meeting | Quarterly |
| QAPI Review | Quarterly |
| Supervisory Visit | Per episode (per patient per discipline) |
| OSHA Review | Annual |
| Infection Control Review | Annual |
| Policy Review | Annual |

## 4. Completing a Compliance Event

Each compliance event has a defined set of steps that must be completed in order.

**To complete an event:**
1. Open the event from the Master Calendar or Dashboard
2. The **Event Workspace** opens, showing all steps in the event's workflow
3. For each step:
   - Read the step instructions
   - Complete the required action (fill a form, upload evidence, get a signature, etc.)
   - Click **Mark Complete** (or the step auto-completes when evidence is submitted)
4. After all steps are complete, submit the event for approval
5. Once approved, the event can be **Certified** (locked as final)

**Step types you may encounter:**
| Step Type | What You Do |
|---|---|
| Task / Action | Manually confirm you completed the action |
| Form Submission | Complete and submit a digital form |
| Evidence Upload | Upload a supporting document |
| Signature Required | Sign the form electronically |
| Approval Required | Wait for a manager's approval decision |

## 5. Uploading Evidence

Evidence documents support your compliance event record.

**To upload evidence:**
1. Open the event's Event Workspace
2. Click the **Evidence Panel** tab (or scroll to the Evidence section)
3. Drag and drop your file into the upload zone, or click **Browse** to select a file
4. Select the **Evidence Kind** from the dropdown (e.g., Meeting Minutes, Attendance Sheet, Completed Form)
5. Click **Upload**

**After uploading:**
- Your evidence status shows as **Submitted** (pending manager review)
- A manager or admin will accept or reject the evidence
- Accepted evidence is locked — it cannot be changed
- If rejected, you will see a rejection reason and can upload a replacement

**Supported file types:** PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG (max 25MB per file)

## 6. Signing a Form Electronically

The eCIgn system lets you sign documents with a legally tracked electronic signature.

**To sign a form:**
1. Navigate to the form requiring your signature (from the event step or the Forms Library)
2. Read the full document before signing
3. Choose your signature method:
   - **Draw** — draw your signature using the mouse or touch screen
   - **Type** — type your full name as a typed signature
4. Click **Sign and Submit**
5. Confirm the signature in the confirmation dialog

**What happens after signing:**
- Your signature is recorded with your identity, role, timestamp, and a document hash (tamper-proof)
- If a second signature is required, the form waits in "Pending Second Signature" state
- Once all signatures are collected, a PDF is generated and the form is complete

**You cannot unsign a completed form** — if an error is made, contact your administrator to void the instance.

## 7. Reviewing Policy Documents

The Policy Library contains all active compliance policies.

**To find a policy:**
1. Navigate to **Policy Library** in the sidebar
2. Use the **Search** bar to search by title or ID
3. Filter by **Domain** (e.g., Clinical, HR, Finance)
4. Filter by **Status** (Draft, Review, Approved, Published)

**To read a policy:**
1. Click the policy title to open the Policy Detail View
2. Read the full text, effective date, and version number
3. Related forms and workflows are listed at the bottom

**Policy Statuses:**
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Work in progress — not effective yet |
| Review | Under review — not yet approved |
| Approved | Approved — awaiting publication |
| Published | Active policy — currently in effect |
| Archived | Superseded — no longer active |

## 8. Using the Forms Library

The Forms Library lists all compliance forms available in the system.

**To access a form:**
1. Navigate to **Forms** in the sidebar
2. Browse the list or use Search
3. Click **Open Form** to fill out and submit a form

**Forms linked to events:** Many forms appear directly in event workflows — you may not need to navigate to the Forms Library directly.

## 9. Using the Global Task Drawer

The Task Drawer is accessible from any page via the task icon in the top navigation bar.

**It shows:**
- Tasks assigned to you
- Personal tasks you have created
- Notifications requiring action

**To create a personal task:**
1. Click the task icon to open the drawer
2. Click **+ Add Task**
3. Enter a title, description, priority, and due date
4. Click **Save**

**Personal tasks are not compliance records** — they are for your own tracking only.

## 10. Completing Onboarding (Journey)

New employees and reinstated staff complete onboarding through the Journey system.

**To access your Journey:**
1. Navigate to **Journey** in the sidebar
2. Your assigned onboarding path appears as a series of modules
3. Click a module to start it
4. Complete all items within the module (policy acknowledgments, video watches, quizzes)
5. Progress is saved automatically as you go

**Journey modules may include:**
- Policy acknowledgment (requires eCIgn signature)
- Video training (watch to completion)
- Quiz assessment (must meet passing score)
- Document uploads (proof of credential, etc.)

**Progress is tracked by HR** — once all modules are complete, HR is notified and your onboarding record is marked complete.

## 11. Checking Your Notifications

**Notifications appear in two places:**
1. The bell icon in the top navigation bar (badge shows unread count)
2. The Global Task Drawer (drawer icon)

**Common notification types:**
| Notification | What to do |
|---|---|
| Task Assigned | Open the task and complete it |
| Signature Required | Navigate to the form and sign |
| Approval Required (manager) | Open the item and approve/reject |
| Event Overdue | Open the event and complete steps immediately |
| Evidence Rejected | Return to the evidence panel and re-upload |

**To dismiss a notification:** Click the notification; it is marked as read automatically when you navigate to the linked item.

## 12. Common Troubleshooting

### "I can't see certain events / policies"
- Your role may not have access. Contact your administrator to verify your role assignment.
- Some events are restricted to specific roles (e.g., Clinical events require `coordinator` role).

### "The Sign button is grayed out"
- It is not your turn to sign — another required signer must sign first.
- Or: The form has already been completed or voided. Check the form status.

### "My evidence upload failed"
- Check that your file is under 25MB and is a supported type (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG).
- If the problem persists, take a screenshot of the error and contact your administrator.

### "I can't mark a step complete"
- A previous step must be completed first — steps must be done in order.
- Or: A required form signature for this step is still pending.

### "The calendar is not loading"
- Refresh the page. If it continues, check your internet connection.
- If the issue persists, the server may be temporarily unavailable; try again in a few minutes.

### "My session expired"
- You were automatically logged out after inactivity (security policy).
- Log back in. Your in-progress work is saved in your browser.
