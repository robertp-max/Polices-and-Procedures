## 2. Purpose

This policy establishes the rules governing the complete lifecycle of every policy document at Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc., from inception through active use, revision, and final archival or sunset. Effective lifecycle control ensures:

- Only currently approved policy versions are in active use — never expired or superseded versions
- All policy reviews are completed on schedule, meeting CMS, OIG, and California regulatory expectations for maintained, current policies
- Version control prevents reliance on outdated procedures that may not reflect current regulatory requirements
- The agency can demonstrate to CMS surveyors, OIG reviewers, CDPH/HCAI inspectors, and accreditation bodies that every policy in its library is current, approved, and has a documented review history
- The policy approval process is appropriately controlled with required executive oversight per GV-PM-001

This policy is the lifecycle management companion to EN-TG-001 (Enterprise Policy Taxonomy & Classification Governance). The taxonomy defines what a policy IS; this policy defines how a policy moves through its stages of existence.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

* All policy documents registered in the Policy Registry under EN-TG-001
* All draft, active, and archived policies across all 10 governance domains
* All Domain Stewards, policy drafters, reviewers, approvers, and publishing custodians
* The Compliance Officer, Administrator, and Governing Body in their oversight roles
* All controlled sub-documents (procedures, checklists, job aids) issued under an authorizing parent policy

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| Lifecycle | The complete arc of a policy document: Creation → Draft → Review → Approval → Active → Scheduled Review → Revision or Reaffirmation → Active (updated) → [Sunset Pending] → Archived. |
| Draft | The initial or revised state of a policy that has not yet been approved. Draft documents shall not be distributed to staff as authoritative guidance. |
| Active | A policy that has been formally approved by the appropriate authority, assigned an effective date, and published for staff use. Only Active policies are operative. |
| Reaffirmation | The formal documentation that a policy has been reviewed on schedule and no changes are required. The policy retains its Policy ID and increments its version number by 0.1 (e.g., v1.0 → v1.1 after reaffirmation). |
| Revision | A change to any substantive element of a policy through the full review and approval process. Revisions increment the version number by a full integer (e.g., v1.0 → v2.0 for significant changes) or 0.1 for minor corrections. |
| Sunset | The intentional termination of a policy, with full documentation of the reason, regulatory basis for termination, and handling of any downstream cross-references. A sunset policy is moved to Archived status. |
| Review Cycle | The maximum interval between scheduled reviews of a policy, based on its Tier and regulatory requirements: REQUIRED-tier policies — Annual; ESSENTIAL-tier — Annual or Biennial; RECOMMENDED-tier — Biennial; GOOD TO HAVE-tier — Triennial. |
| Triggered Review | An out-of-cycle review initiated by: regulatory change, survey finding, incident, significant process change, or Compliance Officer determination. |
| Version History | A permanent record of every version of a policy — the document text at each version, the date, approver, and nature of changes. Retained permanently. |
| Controlled Distribution | The management of which staff receive policy notifications and acknowledgments, and the tracking of policy distribution to ensure no staff member is working with an superseded version. |

## 5. Policy Statement

5.1 Every policy of Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. must be actively maintained through a documented lifecycle. No policy shall remain Active without a completed scheduled review at the interval defined by its Review Cycle.

5.2 Only Active-status policies shall be distributed to staff or referenced as current agency policy. Draft policies are for internal development use only. Archived policies are permanently inaccessible to staff except for historical audit reference.

5.3 All policy revisions must be approved by the appropriate authority per GV-PM-001 (Policy Development & Approval Process) before the revised version becomes Active. The prior version does not expire until the revised version's effective date.

5.4 The effective date of a revised policy shall not precede the approval date. Retroactive policy effective dates are prohibited.

5.5 When a policy is revised or reaffirmed, the prior version is immediately retired to Archived status. The system shall not permit access to a prior version as if it were current.

5.6 Every revision of every policy shall be accompanied by a complete version history entry identifying the nature of changes, the person who approved the revision, and the date.

5.7 Policies overdue for review shall be flagged in the Policy Registry and reported in the monthly summary per EN-TG-001 Section 6.5.2. A REQUIRED-tier policy overdue by more than 60 calendar days beyond its scheduled review date is elevated to a compliance risk requiring Administrator notification.

5.8 This policy itself, as a REQUIRED-tier Enterprise Control policy, shall be reviewed and reaffirmed or revised annually.

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 Policy Creation (Draft Stage)

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.1.1 | Domain Steward / Policy Drafter | Submit a Policy ID Request per EN-TG-001 Section 6.1. Once a Policy ID is assigned, the draft policy is registered in the Policy Registry with Lifecycle Status "Draft" and the draft date. | Before drafting commences. |
| 6.1.2 | Policy Drafter | Prepare the draft policy using the Agency Policy Template (Appendix A), which includes all required metadata fields per EN-TG-001 Section 6.2. The draft shall include preliminary regulatory references and cross-references. | During drafting. |
| 6.1.3 | Domain Steward | Review the draft for: (a) accuracy of regulatory content; (b) completeness of regulatory references; (c) absence of scope overlap with existing Active policies (cross-check Policy Registry); (d) conformance to the policy format template. Return for correction or advance to Compliance Officer review. | Within 14 calendar days of receiving the draft. |
| 6.1.4 | Compliance Officer | Review the draft for: (a) Tier classification accuracy; (b) regulatory compliance; (c) consistency with other policies in the library; (d) cross-reference accuracy. Approve for advancement to approval stage or return for revision. | Within 14 calendar days of receiving the draft from Domain Steward. |

### 6.2 Policy Approval

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.2.1 | Compliance Officer | Route the reviewed draft to the appropriate approval authority per GV-PM-001: (a) REQUIRED-tier policies: Administrator approval; Governing Body notification for policies affecting patient rights, clinical standards, or compliance program; (b) ESSENTIAL-tier: Administrator approval; (c) RECOMMENDED/GOOD TO HAVE: Domain Steward approval countersigned by Compliance Officer. | After Compliance Officer review is complete. |
| 6.2.2 | Approving Authority | Review and sign the policy approval. The approval signature creates the official approval record. Update the Lifecycle Status in the Policy Registry from "Draft" to "Active" upon execution of approval. Record the effective date (must equal or follow the approval date). | Within 14 calendar days of receiving routing. |
| 6.2.3 | Compliance Officer | Upon approval: (a) assign the effective date and Version number (v1.0 for new policies); (b) publish the Active policy to the agency's policy management system; (c) initiate controlled distribution and acknowledgment per GV-PM-003; (d) update the Policy Registry to Active status with current version, effective date, and next review date. | Within 5 business days of approval execution. |
| 6.2.4 | Compliance Officer | Remove all Draft versions of the policy from any circulation channel. Draft markings must not appear on the Active version. | Immediately upon publication of Active version. |

### 6.3 Scheduled Review and Reaffirmation/Revision

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.3.1 | Compliance Officer | Generate a Scheduled Review Report monthly, listing all policies due for review within the next 60 calendar days. Distribute the list to applicable Domain Stewards no later than 45 days before the review due date. | Monthly; distribution 45 days before review due date. |
| 6.3.2 | Domain Steward | Commence review of each assigned policy upon receiving the 45-day notice. Review the policy against: (a) any regulatory changes since last review (per CO-RA-001); (b) any audit findings, incident investigations, or corrective actions affecting the policy's procedures; (c) any operational changes that affect the policy scope. | Commence within 5 business days of 45-day notice. |
| 6.3.3 | Domain Steward | Determine outcome: (a) No Changes Required → prepare Reaffirmation Memo (Appendix B) documenting the review checklist outcome and confirming no changes are warranted; (b) Changes Required → initiate the revision process per Section 6.4 with a marked-up draft. | Within 21 calendar days of commencing review. |
| 6.3.4 | Compliance Officer | Execute Reaffirmation or advance Revision per outcome: (a) Reaffirmation — co-sign Reaffirmation Memo with Domain Steward; update Policy Registry: increment version by 0.1, update Last Reviewed date, set Next Review Date. The policy content is unchanged; (b) Revision — advance to Section 6.4. | Within 7 calendar days of receiving reaffirmation memo or revised draft. |
| 6.3.5 | Compliance Officer | Flag any policy not reaffirmed or revised by its scheduled review date as Overdue in the Policy Registry. Immediately notify the Domain Steward and Administrator. For REQUIRED-tier: escalate to Administrator; 60-day overdue = compliance risk per Section 5.7. | On the business day following the review due date. |

### 6.4 Revision Process

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.4.1 | Domain Steward | Prepare the revised draft using tracked changes against the current Active version. Clearly identify every change: (a) the specific text changed; (b) the reason for the change (regulatory update, incident finding, process change, other); (c) the regulatory or operational basis for the change. | Within 14 calendar days of determining revision is needed. |
| 6.4.2 | Compliance Officer | Review the revised draft for: (a) regulatory accuracy; (b) consistency with other library policies; (c) completeness of version history entry; (d) cross-reference updates required as a result of this revision. | Within 14 calendar days of receiving revised draft. |
| 6.4.3 | Approving Authority | Approve the revised policy per the Tier-based approval authority in Section 6.2.1. Sign and date. | Within 14 calendar days of receiving for approval. |
| 6.4.4 | Compliance Officer | Upon approval of revision: (a) assign the new version number (increment by 1.0 for substantive changes, 0.1 for minor corrections); (b) set the effective date; (c) publish the revised Active version; (d) immediately archive the prior version; (e) update the Policy Registry — version, effective date, next review date; (f) initiate re-acknowledgment process per GV-PM-003 for staff affected by the change; (g) update Version History log (Appendix C). | Within 5 business days of approval. |
| 6.4.5 | Compliance Officer | After publishing the revision: review all policies that cross-reference this policy. If a cross-reference is now inaccurate (e.g., the referenced section number changed), flag the cross-referencing policies for correction at their next scheduled review or sooner if the cross-reference inaccuracy creates a compliance risk. | Within 30 days of revision publication. |

### 6.5 Triggered Review

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.5.1 | Compliance Officer or Domain Steward | Initiate a Triggered Review when any of the following occur: (a) a regulatory change affects the policy content (per CO-RA-001); (b) a CMS, CDPH, or Cal/OSHA survey finding cites a procedure covered by the policy; (c) an incident investigation RCA (QA-AE-002) identifies a policy-level deficiency; (d) a significant operational change renders a policy's procedures non-operational. | Within 14 calendar days of triggering event. |
| 6.5.2 | Compliance Officer | Document the Triggered Review in the Policy Registry: note the trigger event, the initiating party, and the date. Update Lifecycle Status to "Under Triggered Review." | At initiation. |
| 6.5.3 | Domain Steward | Complete the triggered revision within 30 calendar days of the trigger event (or per regulatory/survey response timeline if sooner required). Advance through approval per Section 6.4 on expedited timeline. | Within 30 calendar days of trigger (or shorter if survey-directed). |

### 6.6 Policy Sunset and Archival

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.6.1 | Compliance Officer or Domain Steward | Initiate a sunset action when: (a) the regulatory basis for a REQUIRED-tier policy is eliminated; (b) the policy is redundant due to consolidation into another policy; (c) the policy covers a service or function the agency has formally discontinued. Document the sunset rationale in the Sunset Request Form (Appendix D). | At identification; submit form to Compliance Officer. |
| 6.6.2 | Compliance Officer | Review the sunset request: (a) verify no active regulatory requirement still mandates the policy; (b) verify all cross-references to the policy are updated or will be updated to remove the reference; (c) verify no downstream controlled documents (checklists, job aids) depend solely on the sunsetted policy. | Within 14 calendar days of receiving sunset request. |
| 6.6.3 | Administrator | Approve the sunset action. The Governing Body shall be notified at the next scheduled meeting for any REQUIRED-tier policy sunset. | Administrator approval within 14 days; Governing Body notification at next meeting. |
| 6.6.4 | Compliance Officer | Upon approval of sunset: (a) remove the Active policy from staff access; (b) update Policy Registry status to "Archived"; (c) retain the archived policy (all versions) permanently in the policy archive; (d) update any other policies that cross-reference the sunsetted policy within 30 days. | Within 3 business days of approval; cross-reference updates within 30 days. |

### 6.7 Escalation and Exception Handling

| Condition | Escalation Path | Corrective Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| REQUIRED-tier policy overdue for review by 60+ days | Compliance Officer escalates to Administrator by written memo. | Administrator directs Domain Steward to complete review within 14 days. If Domain Steward resource capacity is insufficient, Administrator reallocates resources. | Escalation: immediately at 60-day mark. Completion required: 14 days from escalation. |
| Superseded policy found circulating to staff | Any employee may report to Compliance Officer or Domain Steward. | Compliance Officer removes document from circulation within 2 business days. Retraining issued to staff who may have relied on the superseded version. | Removal: 2 business days. Retraining: within 14 days. |
| Approval authority is unavailable for a REQUIRED revision needed within a regulatory deadline | Administrator designates alternate approver per GV-OG-005 (Delegation of Authority). | Alternate approver reviews and approves in emergency capacity. Document the basis for alternate approval in the Version History log. | Alternate approval executed within the regulatory deadline. |

## 8. Compliance & Audit Considerations

### 8.1 How Compliance Is Measured

| Compliance Indicator | Measurement Method | Acceptable Standard |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| All REQUIRED-tier policies reviewed within their scheduled cycle | Registry audit — days overdue count for each policy | 0 REQUIRED-tier policies overdue beyond their review date |
| All policy revisions have complete version history entries | Audit of Version History Log for each revised policy | 100% of revisions have complete version history |
| No superseded policy versions in active staff circulation | Random audit of documents in staff circulation | 0 superseded versions in circulation |
| Reaffirmation memos on file for policies not needing revision | Review of reaffirmation memo file vs. policies due for review | 100% of due-for-review policies have either reaffirmation memo or revision documentation |
| Policy Registry is updated within 7 calendar days of lifecycle actions | Spot audit of registry update dates vs. policy action dates | 100% within 7 calendar days |

### 8.2 What Surveyors and Auditors Will Look For

CMS surveyors and OIG reviewers will specifically assess:

1. Whether the agency's policies are current — they will request the most recent version of key policies and compare effective dates and version numbers against internal records. A policy unchanged for 5+ years without a reaffirmation memo is suspect.
2. Whether the agency has a documented process for identifying when policies need revision — co-RA-001 regulatory monitoring tied to policy lifecycle triggers is essential.
3. Whether a superseded version of a policy was in use at the time of a care episode in question. This is critical in deficiency investigations where surveyors ask "what was your policy at the time?"
4. Whether significant changes (like a new regulation) were promptly incorporated into policy documents.
5. Whether staff were notified of policy changes (acknowledgment records per GV-PM-003).

### 8.3 Common Failure Points

| Failure Point | Risk | Mitigation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Policies not reviewed on schedule — no system for tracking overdue reviews | CMS cites lack of policy maintenance; OIG compliance concern | Compliance Officer generates monthly scheduled review report; 45-day advance notice system |
| No version history — cannot demonstrate what changed when | Cannot defend policy history in survey deficiency review | Version History Log (Appendix C) required for every policy from v1.0 |
| Superseded policy found at patient bedside or in field bag | Creates confusion about what clinical standard was operative | Controlled distribution + staff acknowledgment per GV-PM-003 ensures only current versions are distributed |
| Reaffirmation not documented — review was conducted verbally | Surveyor treats unreviewed policy as out-of-date | Reaffirmation Memo (Appendix B) is required for all scheduled reviews, even for no-change outcomes |
| REQUIRED-tier policy overdue without escalation | Compliance program gap; regulatory risk | 60-day overdue automatic escalation to Administrator per Section 5.7 |
