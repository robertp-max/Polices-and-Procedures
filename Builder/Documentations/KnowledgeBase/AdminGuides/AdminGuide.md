# Admin Guide

## Managing Users
- Use admin user/role assignment pages to provision and maintain user access.
- Validate account lifecycle states and onboarding readiness before granting elevated capabilities.

## Roles and Permissions
- Review role catalog and permission mapping.
- Apply least-privilege assignment principles.
- Validate role changes against operational and compliance responsibilities.

## Assignments
- Assign tasks and workflow responsibilities by role and owner.
- Monitor assignment backlog, due dates, and completion quality.

## Workflow Oversight
- Track workflow state transitions and blocked dependencies.
- Confirm required form/signature gates are enforced.
- Escalate stalled execution paths with auditable notes.

## Audit Review
- Use audit/evidence views to verify append-only trace continuity.
- Confirm policy_id, workflow_id, and event_id lineage exists for regulated actions.
- Investigate and resolve traceability gaps promptly.
