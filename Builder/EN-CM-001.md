## 2. Purpose

This policy establishes the enterprise compliance metrics program for Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. It defines the indicators, measurement methodology, reporting infrastructure, and governance accountability required to continuously measure the agency's compliance posture across all 10 governance domains.

Effective compliance metrics enable the agency to:

- Detect emerging compliance risk before it becomes a regulatory finding or civil liability
- Provide the Governing Body with quantitative evidence that the Compliance Program is functioning, as required by OIG Compliance Element 5 (Monitoring and Auditing)
- Demonstrate to CMS surveyors and CDPH that the agency actively measures, reports, and acts on compliance performance data
- Link QAPI's data-driven quality improvement mandate (42 CFR §484.65) to compliance-specific performance tracking
- Identify domains or subdomains where policy adherence is consistently low, triggering targeted corrective action

This policy is companion to EN-TG-001 (what the policy library is) and EN-LC-001 (how policies are maintained current). Together, the three policies in the EN domain constitute the enterprise governance and control framework that makes the agency's compliance program auditable, measurable, and defensible.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

* All 10 governance domains and their respective Domain Stewards
* The Compliance Officer as program owner and data custodian
* The Quality Assurance Director as co-steward for QAPI-linked metrics (42 CFR §484.65)
* The Administrator and Governing Body as receiving authorities for Compliance Metrics Reports
* All staff, supervisors, and managers whose activities generate compliance metric inputs
* All CMS, OIG, California, and accreditation compliance obligations of the agency

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| Compliance Metric | A quantifiable measure of the agency's adherence to a specific regulatory requirement, policy standard, or compliance program element. |
| Enterprise Compliance Score (ECS) | An aggregate percentage-based score representing the agency's overall compliance posture across all domains for a reporting period, calculated as: (total compliant indicators ÷ total indicators measured) × 100. |
| Domain Compliance Score (DCS) | The ECS calculated at the domain level (e.g., CL domain, GV domain), representing compliance performance within one governance domain. |
| Key Performance Indicator (KPI) | A selected metric designated as an agency priority for regular monitoring. KPIs are tracked every reporting period; non-KPI metrics may be tracked quarterly or annually. |
| Threshold | The minimum acceptable compliance score for a metric or domain. Breach of threshold triggers escalation and corrective action. |
| Green / Yellow / Red Rating | Status classifications applied to each metric: Green = at or above threshold (compliant); Yellow = 90–99% of threshold (warning — monitor closely); Red = below threshold (deficient — corrective action required). |
| Compliance Dashboard | The agency's internal reporting tool — a structured display of all KPIs with current scores, trend data, and Green/Yellow/Red status designations. Updated monthly and provided to the Administrator. |
| Baseline | The initial measured value of a metric upon first collection, used as the reference point for improvement trending. |
| Regulatory Audit Trail | The documented evidence that a compliance metric was measured, the data source used, the result, and any responsive action taken. Required to demonstrate OIG Compliance Element 5. |
| Corrective Action Threshold | The pre-defined score level at which a metric automatically triggers a formal CAP under QA-AE-003. For REQUIRED-tier compliance metrics: 90%; for ESSENTIAL-tier: 85%. |

## 5. Policy Statement

5.1 Care Indeed Home Health Care, Inc. shall maintain an active, data-driven Enterprise Compliance Metrics Program operated by the Compliance Officer. The program shall measure compliance KPIs across all 10 governance domains on a continuous basis.

5.2 Each governance domain shall have defined KPIs. The minimum number of active KPIs per domain is three (3). REQUIRED-tier compliance areas shall always have at least one KPI each. KPIs are approved by the Compliance Officer and documented in the KPI Registry (Appendix A).

5.3 Compliance metrics shall be reported on the following cadence: monthly dashboard to Administrator; quarterly Compliance Metrics Summary to Compliance Committee; semi-annual report to Governing Body. The semi-annual Governing Body report shall include: trend analysis, domains below threshold, open CAPs with status, and comparative analysis vs. prior period.

5.4 When any compliance metric falls below its corrective action threshold, the Compliance Officer shall initiate a formal Corrective Action Plan (CAP) per QA-AE-003 within 14 calendar days.

5.5 The ECS and all Domain Compliance Scores shall be incorporated into the agency's annual QAPI Program Evaluation per 42 CFR §484.65(b)(5), with specific analysis of whether any compliance metric deficiency correlated with a patient care quality issue.

5.6 Compliance data is confidential — access is limited to the Compliance Officer, Administrator, Governing Body members, Domain Stewards (their own domain data), and authorized auditors. Compliance metrics data shall not be shared with staff below the supervisory level except in aggregate, anonymized, educational formats.

5.7 This policy shall be reviewed annually. The KPI Registry (Appendix A) shall be reviewed and updated at each annual cycle to add new regulatory KPIs, retire obsolete KPIs, and adjust thresholds based on performance history.

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 KPI Definition and KPI Registry

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.1.1 | Compliance Officer | Identify minimum KPIs for each domain. For each KPI, define: (a) KPI ID (e.g., EN-KPI-01); (b) description; (c) regulatory basis; (d) data source; (e) measurement method; (f) reporting frequency; (g) threshold (and corrective action threshold); (h) Green/Yellow/Red definitions; (i) responsible party for data collection. | At initial program adoption; update at each annual review. |
| 6.1.2 | Compliance Officer | Publish the KPI Registry (Appendix A) to the Policy Registry. The KPI Registry is a governing document protected at Tier 2 access. | Within 30 days of initial program adoption; within 30 days of each annual update. |
| 6.1.3 | Compliance Officer | When a new regulatory requirement is identified per CO-RA-001, assess whether a new KPI is needed to measure ongoing compliance with that requirement. If yes, add to KPI Registry via the annual update cycle, or immediately for emergency regulatory changes. | Within 30 days of regulatory change identification (or annual cycle). |

### 6.2 Baseline Measurement Collection

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.2.1 | Compliance Officer (with Domain Stewards) | Collect baseline data for each KPI upon adoption of this program. Document the baseline value, the data collection date, the data source, and any limitations noted (e.g., incomplete records for the prior period). Record in the KPI Registry Baseline column. | Within 60 days of program adoption. |
| 6.2.2 | Domain Stewards | Provide Compliance Officer with access to domain-specific data sources (audit logs, training records, incident reports, policy acknowledgment records, chart review results) needed for KPI measurement. | Ongoing; within 5 business days of any Compliance Officer data request. |

### 6.3 Monthly Compliance Dashboard Production

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.3.1 | Compliance Officer | By the 10th calendar day of each month, collect data for all monthly-frequency KPIs from applicable data sources. Document the data point for each KPI for the preceding month. | By the 10th of each month. |
| 6.3.2 | Compliance Officer | Calculate scores for each KPI: (measured compliant instances ÷ total measured instances) × 100. Apply Green/Yellow/Red status per KPI threshold definitions. | By the 12th of each month. |
| 6.3.3 | Compliance Officer | Calculate Domain Compliance Scores and Enterprise Compliance Score: (a) Domain Compliance Score = average score across all KPIs in the domain; (b) Enterprise Compliance Score = average score across all domains. | By the 12th of each month. |
| 6.3.4 | Compliance Officer | Produce the Monthly Compliance Dashboard (Appendix B template): (a) ECS and trend (vs. prior month); (b) domain-level table with DCS and trend; (c) KPI detail table for each domain — KPI, score, rating, trend, open CAPs; (d) any metrics newly entering Yellow or Red status; (e) escalation summary for metrics at Red status. | By the 15th of each month. |
| 6.3.5 | Compliance Officer | Distribute the Monthly Compliance Dashboard to the Administrator. Retain a copy in the compliance management record. | By the 15th of each month. |

### 6.4 Quarterly Compliance Metrics Summary

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.4.1 | Compliance Officer | Produce a Quarterly Compliance Metrics Summary at the close of each calendar quarter (Q1: Jan–Mar, Q2: Apr–Jun, Q3: Jul–Sep, Q4: Oct–Dec). The Summary shall include: (a) quarterly-average ECS and DCS; (b) trend analysis vs. prior quarter; (c) metrics that remained Red for ≥2 consecutive months; (d) open Corrective Action Plans with progress status; (e) new metrics added to the KPI Registry this quarter; (f) upcoming regulatory compliance obligations and associated KPI watch. | Within 30 days of quarter close. |
| 6.4.2 | Compliance Officer | Present the Quarterly Summary to the Compliance Committee (or Administrator if no formal committee exists). Document meeting attendance and discussion in meeting minutes. | Within 30 days of quarter close. |

### 6.5 Semi-Annual Governing Body Compliance Report

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.5.1 | Compliance Officer | Prepare the Semi-Annual Governing Body Compliance Report, which shall include: (a) 6-month ECS trend by month; (b) domain-level performance summary for all 10 domains; (c) identification of any domain scoring below threshold for ≥2 consecutive months; (d) all open CAPs — description, responsible party, target date, current status; (e) CAPs closed this period — description, outcome, and whether metrics improved; (f) regulatory changes incorporated this period and KPIs added in response; (g) upcoming regulatory compliance milestones; (h) Compliance Officer certification that the Compliance Program is functioning per OIG Element 5. | 30 days before each Governing Body semi-annual meeting. |
| 6.5.2 | Administrator | Present the Semi-Annual Report to the Governing Body. Document Governing Body discussion, questions, and any directives in Governing Body meeting minutes per GV-GB-001. | At Governing Body meeting. |
| 6.5.3 | Governing Body Chair | Acknowledge receipt of the Semi-Annual Compliance Report in meeting minutes. If any domain has been below threshold for ≥2 quarters, the Governing Body directs corrective action and requests a status update at the next meeting. | At and following Governing Body meeting. |

### 6.6 Corrective Action Triggers and Response

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.6.1 | Compliance Officer | Upon identifying any metric at Red status (below corrective action threshold): (a) notify the Domain Steward immediately; (b) initiate a formal CAP per QA-AE-003 within 14 calendar days; (c) document the CAP in the Compliance Dashboard's open CAP tracking section. | CAP initiated within 14 calendar days of metric falling to Red status. |
| 6.6.2 | Domain Steward | In collaboration with Compliance Officer: (a) conduct a root cause analysis (RCA) of the compliance failure; (b) propose corrective actions with specific tasks, responsible parties, and target dates; (c) implement corrective actions; (d) provide progress updates to Compliance Officer monthly. | RCA within 30 days; corrective action implementation per CAP timeline. |
| 6.6.3 | Compliance Officer | Monitor the metric that triggered the CAP monthly after implementation of corrective actions. Document whether the metric returns to Green status. If after 3 months of corrective action the metric has not improved to ≥yellow status, escalate to Administrator and consider whether the CAP needs redesign. | Monthly post-CAP monitoring; 3-month escalation if no improvement. |
| 6.6.4 | Compliance Officer | When a CAP is closed successfully (metric returns to Green for ≥2 consecutive months), document the closure: (a) final metric scores; (b) corrective actions implemented; (c) sustainability plan to prevent regression; (d) notify the Governing Body at next semi-annual report. | Upon achievement of 2 consecutive Green months. |

### 6.7 QAPI Integration

| Step | Responsible Party | Action | Timeframe |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 6.7.1 | Compliance Officer / QA Director | At the start of each QAPI cycle (quarterly), the Compliance Officer shares the compliance metrics data with the QA Director for integration into the QAPI performance dataset per QA-PG-001 and QA-PG-002. | At the start of each QAPI quarter. |
| 6.7.2 | QA Director | Assess whether any compliance KPI at Yellow or Red status corresponds to a patient care quality indicator. If a compliance deficit correlates with care quality risk, initiate a joint QAPI-Compliance review process. Document the correlation analysis in the QAPI record. | Within 30 days of receiving compliance metrics data each quarter. |
| 6.7.3 | Compliance Officer | Incorporate the compliance metrics program performance into the agency's Annual QAPI Program Evaluation per 42 CFR §484.65(b)(5). The evaluation shall specifically address whether the compliance program has contributed to quality improvement outcomes. | At annual QAPI Program Evaluation. |

## 8. Compliance & Audit Considerations

### 8.1 How Compliance Is Measured

| Compliance Indicator | Measurement Method | Acceptable Standard |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Monthly dashboards produced on time | Audit of dashboard production dates vs. 15th of month standard | 100% within allotted timeframes; 0 missed months |
| All Red-status metrics have active CAPs | Audit of open CAPs vs. count of Red-status metrics | 100% of Red-status metrics have an associated CAP within 14 days |
| Governing Body received semi-annual compliance report | Governing Body meeting minutes showing report received and acknowledged | 100% per year (two reports per year minimum) |
| KPI Registry reviewed and updated annually | Version history of KPI Registry document | Annual update documented; 0 years without update |
| QAPI compliance integration documented each quarter | QAPI records showing compliance data integration | 100% of quarters with documented integration |

### 8.2 What Surveyors and Auditors Will Look For

CMS surveyors and OIG reviewers conducting compliance program assessments will ask:

1. How does the agency know its compliance program is working? This policy is the answer — quantitative KPI measurement provides the evidence.
2. What happens when a compliance metric is deficient? The CAP→QA-AE-003 pathway with Governing Body oversight demonstrates accountability.
3. Has the Governing Body been informed of compliance performance status? Semi-annual reports with Governing Body meeting minutes prove this.
4. Is QAPI integrated with compliance monitoring? The QAPI integration procedure (Section 6.7) and documented cross-references to 42 CFR §484.65 provide this evidence.
5. What is the agency's Enterprise Compliance Score? The Compliance Officer should be able to produce the most recent Monthly Dashboard immediately on request.

### 8.3 Common Failure Points

| Failure Point | Risk | Mitigation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Compliance program exists on paper but metrics are not actually measured | OIG Element 5 failure — monitoring and auditing is not being performed | KPI Registry with defined data sources, responsible parties, and measurement methods makes measurement concrete and auditable |
| Red-status metrics identified but no corrective action initiated | Survey finding: agency failed to act on known compliance deficiency — highest risk condition | Automatic CAP initiation at 14 days when a metric hits Red |
| Governing Body not receiving compliance reports | Survey finding: Governing Body lacks meaningful compliance oversight, violating its 42 CFR §484.105 responsibilities | Semi-annual report schedule with mandatory Governing Body meeting minutes documentation |
| Compliance metrics siloed from QAPI — parallel but disconnected programs | Missed opportunity to link compliance deficits to care quality risks; 42 CFR §484.65 integration requirement missed | Formal QAPI integration procedure (Section 6.7) with documentation requirement |
| KPI thresholds set too low to drive improvement | Meets the paper standard but does not identify meaningful compliance risk | Initial thresholds set at 95% for REQUIRED-tier metrics; reviewed annually for adjustment |
| No trend data — each month's data is standalone | Cannot identify gradual compliance deterioration before it becomes a Red crisis | Monthly Dashboard includes trend (vs. 3-month rolling average) for every KPI |
