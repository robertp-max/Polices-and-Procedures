# KB-001 — Getting Started in CES

**Audience:** Any new user. **Time to read:** 3 minutes.

## What CES is

The Compliance Execution Sprint System (CES) is where you do — and prove —
your compliance work. Every regulatory deadline, audit, signature, and
evidence file in the agency runs through CES.

## First login

1. Open the app, sign in with your agency credentials.
2. You will land on the **CES Dashboard**.
3. The left sidebar shows the 8 main sections:
   Dashboard · Calendar · Sprint Board · Workflows · Master Controls ·
   Audit Mode · Evidence Center · Reports.

## What to do today

1. Click **Sprint Board**.
2. Filter to "My Work" (top-right) — you will see only units assigned to
   you in the active 14-day sprint.
3. Pick the unit with the closest due date and open it.
4. Read the **Workflow Phase** badge — that tells you what step to do.
5. Follow the in-drawer checklist; the system will tell you exactly what
   it needs to advance.

## What to never do

- Don't try to add an arbitrary task — CES only accepts work that comes
  from the regulatory calendar.
- Don't skip phases. The system enforces preparation → documentation →
  review → signature → audit in that order.
- Don't mark complete before evidence is filed.
