FORM ID: CO-FM-014
TITLE: Breach Risk Assessment Worksheet
TYPE: Worksheet
DOMAIN: CO
USAGE: Conditional
FREQUENCY: Triggered
VERSION: 6.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-07-10
NEXT REVIEW DATE: 2026-07-10
ORIENTATION: portrait
CLASSIFICATIONS: audit_critical, high_risk

LINKED POLICIES:
- CO-HP-003
- CO-IR-101

PURPOSE:
Four-factor breach risk assessment per 45 CFR § 164.402 to determine whether an impermissible use/disclosure of PHI constitutes a reportable breach.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Completed by the Privacy Officer within 5 business days of incident discovery. All four factors must be addressed.

SECTION 1: Section 1 — Identification
layout: grid

fields:
- Form Completed By (Full Name) (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Title / Role (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Department / Branch (type=text, col=2)
- Date Completed (type=date, required=true, col=2)

SECTION 2: Section 2 — Worksheet
layout: grid

fields:
- Incident ID / Date (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Assessor / Privacy Officer (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Factor 1 — Nature and Extent of PHI Involved (type=textarea, required=true, col=4)
- Factor 2 — Unauthorized Person Who Used or Received the PHI (type=textarea, required=true, col=4)
- Factor 3 — Whether PHI Was Actually Acquired or Viewed (type=textarea, required=true, col=4)
- Factor 4 — Extent to Which Risk Has Been Mitigated (type=textarea, required=true, col=4)
- Conclusion — Reportable Breach? (Y/N) (type=select, required=true, col=2, options=[Yes — Reportable | No — Low probability of compromise])
- Basis for Determination (type=textarea, col=4)

SECTION 3: Section — Signatures & Attestation
layout: signature

fields:
- Privacy Officer — Printed Name (type=text, col=2)
- Privacy Officer — Signature (type=signature, col=1)
- Privacy Officer — Date (type=date, col=1)
- Compliance Officer — Printed Name (type=text, col=2)
- Compliance Officer — Signature (type=signature, col=1)
- Compliance Officer — Date (type=date, col=1)
