PURPOSE:
Official agency organizational chart establishing reporting relationships from the Governing Body through senior clinical and administrative leadership as required by 42 CFR § 484.105.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Updated within 7 calendar days of any reorganization or leadership change. Posted in the Administrator's office and included in every surveyor packet.

- Date Completed (type=date, required=true, col=2)
