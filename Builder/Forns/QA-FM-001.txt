PURPOSE:
Quarterly QAPI Committee meeting minutes template per 42 CFR § 484.65(b), documenting data review, improvement projects, and board reporting.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Minutes finalized within 5 business days. Aggregate report provided to Governing Body.
