PURPOSE:
Quarterly phishing simulation report — sent volume, click rate, credential-entry rate, report rate, training assignment for failures, and trend over quarters.

INSTRUCTIONS:
IT SecOps runs; HR coordinates training for repeat clickers; trend reported to IT/Security Committee (IT-FM-031).
