PURPOSE:
Formal authorization form executed before destruction of any record (physical or electronic) that has reached the end of its retention period. Confirms no active legal hold applies and documents method, date, witness, and certification of destruction. Supports HIPAA § 164.530(j) and 42 CFR § 484.110 records-retention compliance.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Requestor (Records Custodian) completes Sections 1–2 and routes to Compliance Officer and Legal Counsel for clearance (Section 3). Destruction is performed under witness and certified in Section 4. Destroyed per approved method (NIST 800-88 for media; HIPAA-compliant shredding for paper). Retain authorization 10 years post-destruction.
