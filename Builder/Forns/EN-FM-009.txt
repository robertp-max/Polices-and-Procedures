PURPOSE:
Immutable version-control log for every published policy — records each version, effective date, approver, and summary of changes for permanent audit retention.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Automatically appended by the Policy Administrator at each approved revision. Entries are never deleted; superseded entries are marked as "Archived."
