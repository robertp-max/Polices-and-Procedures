PURPOSE:
Hazardous materials (Safety Data Sheet) inventory for each branch and field location, per OSHA Hazard Communication Standard.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Maintained by Safety Officer. SDS accessible to all workforce within 10 seconds.

SECTION 1: Section 1 — Identification
layout: grid

fields:
- Form Completed By (Full Name) (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Title / Role (type=text, required=true, col=2)
- Department / Branch (type=text, col=2)
- Date Completed (type=date, required=true, col=2)

SECTION 2: Section 2 — Log Entries
layout: table

table_columns:
- Product Name
- Manufacturer
- Location
- SDS Date
- Quantity
- Storage
- Disposal Method
table_row_count: 15

SECTION 3: Section — Signatures & Attestation
layout: signature

fields:
- Completed By — Printed Name (type=text, col=2)
- Completed By — Signature (type=signature, col=1)
- Completed By — Date (type=date, col=1)
- Supervisor / Reviewer — Printed Name (type=text, col=2)
- Supervisor / Reviewer — Signature (type=signature, col=1)
- Supervisor / Reviewer — Date (type=date, col=1)
