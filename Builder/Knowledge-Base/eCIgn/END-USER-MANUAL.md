# eCIgn — End User Manual

**Audience:** Coordinators, Approvers, Managers, Auditors using the application.

---

## B1. How to open assigned form tasks
1. Open **My Tasks** from the left navigation.
2. Tasks are grouped by event. Status chip shows where each task stands.
3. Click any task row to open the **Right Panel** with full detail.

## B2. How to complete a form
1. From the Right Panel, click **Open form workspace**.
2. The eCIgn workspace launches with the disclosure / verification / review / attestation steps in order.
3. Move through each step using the on-screen prompts. The system records each step automatically.

## B3. How to save a draft
- Drafts save automatically as you advance steps.
- You can leave the workspace at any time; the packet status shows **Draft** until disclosure is acknowledged.

## B4. How to submit a form
- After the **review** step, click **Submit for signature**. The packet moves to **Awaiting signature**.

## B5. How to sign a form
1. From My Tasks, open the task with status **Awaiting signature**.
2. Click **Open form workspace** → **Sign**.
3. Provide MFA verification when prompted.
4. After all required signers complete, the system locks the packet and generates evidence.

## B6. How to request a correction (Approver)
- When reviewing a locked packet, choose **Return for correction** and enter the reason.
- The packet moves to **Returned for correction** and reappears in the original Coordinator's My Tasks.

## B7. How to approve or reject (Approver)
- Open the packet from My Tasks and choose **Approve** or **Reject** with a written reason.
- Approved packets generate validated evidence and the linked task moves to **Done** in all PM views.

## B8. How to find completed evidence
- From the Right Panel of a completed task, the **Evidence** section shows the evidence ID and storage location.
- Auditors can also browse the **Evidence Center** page to see all evidence linked to events.

## B9. How to track task status
- **My Tasks** lists everything assigned to you.
- The **Event View** shows tasks grouped by event.
- A single task always shows the same `task_id` and the same content in any view (the Right Panel is shared).

## B10. Common mistakes to avoid
- **Don't try to mark a task complete in PM** — completion only occurs when eCIgn locks + (if required) approves.
- **Don't schedule compliance work on Sat/Sun** unless a manager provides an explicit override and reason.
- **Don't sign on someone else's behalf** — every signer is verified individually.
- **Don't bypass the eCIgn workspace** by attaching evidence elsewhere — it will not be tied to the packet hash chain.
