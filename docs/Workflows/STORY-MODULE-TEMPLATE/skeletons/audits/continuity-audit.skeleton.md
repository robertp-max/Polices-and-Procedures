# Skeleton — audits/continuity-audit.md

**Purpose:** the story holds together — within the module, and against the shared universe.
**Exemplar:** `docs/GAO-001-A-New-Journey/audits/continuity-audit.md`

## Required sections

1. **Cast consistency** — every character's name, role, and facts consistent across all scenes and against `03-STORY-UNIVERSE.md`. Flags: unnamed recurring characters, contradicted canon, story-bible characters who never appear (decide: cut from bible or add an appearance).
2. **Timeline & causality** — story time advances coherently; scenes that continue a prior scene's situation (same visit, same day) keep every detail consistent.
3. **Props & devices** — continuity devices used per the bible; no prop drift (an object introduced one way reappearing differently), no re-introduction of established props.
4. **Terminology & voice** — retired terms don't resurface (GAO-001 example: old pillar names in the recap); Field Notes stay in-voice (flag textbook-voice lapses); signature phrases match the universe registry verbatim-in-spirit.
5. **Cross-module canon check** — nothing contradicts other converted modules' packages (check the universe registry's timeline table).
6. **Findings** — numbered, severity-rated, fix-located, resolution-proposed (same actionability bar as the coverage audit).

## Done when

Every scene pair that shares cast/time/props has been checked; every finding is fixable without interpretation.
