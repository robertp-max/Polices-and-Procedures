# Storyboard Continuity Audit — GAO-001 "A New Journey"

## 1. Cast / Name / Motif Inconsistencies

**1a. Supervisor named twice, inconsistently — CONFIRMED HIGH SEVERITY**
The character sheet fixes the preceptor as **Dana Whitfield, RN**, named explicitly throughout Scenes 1, 2 (partially), 6, 7 (partially), 8, and 9. But Scene 2's storyboard body never names her once — every reference is "the supervisor" ("the supervisor doesn't recite the mission statement," "the supervisor taps the first phrase," etc.), across the Story Beat, Learner Role, and the entire Narration Plan. Scene 3 correctly uses "Dana." This means Scene 2 — the mission briefing, arguably Dana's signature scene per the bible's continuity-devices section ("Dana's voice should feel like the same person throughout... used first in Scene 2's mission table") — never actually identifies her by name. A learner hits "Dana" in Scenes 1/3/6/7/8/9 and an unnamed "supervisor" in Scene 2, breaking the named-character throughline exactly where the bible says her voice should first anchor.

**1b. Patient names dropped/altered — CONFIRMED**
- Character sheet names Scene 4's patient **Mr. Sam Okafor**, used correctly throughout Scene 4, and referenced correctly in Scenes 5 and 9's bridges. Good.
- Character sheet names Scene 7's patient **Mr. Ray Torres**, and Scene 7 uses "Mr. Torres" / "Ray Torres" throughout — consistent.
- Scene 8, Branch 2 (the bruising scenario), is supposed to be the **same** Mr. Torres per the Story Arc's Week Structure table (Scene 8 row references "Scenario: Mrs. Chen's daughter's request" only, but the Character Sheet explicitly assigns Torres to "Scene 7 (bruising/observation scenario)"). Scene 8 as drafted, however, **never names the Branch 2 patient at all** — it's "an older patient's home (composite, staged in the anchor image's living room)," "the patient," "the older patient." This is a real gap: Scene 7 already spent a full scene establishing Ray Torres as the bruising-observation patient, and Scene 8's own Story Beat says Branch 2 is "later that afternoon at an older patient's home" as if it's a *different* stop — but the learning-node table's Source Coverage column cites "Page 8 Scenario 1 (retained/transferred)," implying it's meant to be the *same* canonical scenario Scene 7 uses. Scene 8 either (a) is unintentionally duplicating Torres's bruising beat as a second, unnamed patient on the same day, or (b) intends a different patient but the character sheet only budgeted one bruising patient (Torres) for the whole week. Either way the anonymization breaks the named-cast continuity every other scene maintains, and Scene 9's recap ("the patient from the reporting scenario") lampshades this exact ambiguity rather than resolving it.
- Character sheet names Scene 8's other patient **Mrs. Linh Chen** and her daughter **Grace Chen**. Scene 8 as drafted never names either — "Mrs. Chen's daughter," "the daughter," no first names anywhere. Minor but consistent with the pattern of Scene 8 quietly de-naming characters the bible explicitly named.
- Scene 9's editorial note goes further and **retroactively strips names from Scenes 6/8 recap copy** ("the patient from the reporting scenario," "the family member's off-scope request") because "those scenes' approved storyboards were not available at authoring time" — but Scenes 6, 7, and 8 *are* in this same document, already drafted, already naming Torres and Chen. Scene 9 was written as if blind to content that in fact already exists in the same package.

**1c. Badge and notebook motifs dropped exactly where the bible mandates them — CONFIRMED**
- The bible requires the badge to be referenced in Scenes 6 and 9 "you'll wear this into every home." Scene 6's narration/Field Notes never mention the badge at all until Scene 6's own drafted text ("Dana glances at the badge clipped to Alex's scrub top... 'That badge means something...'") — actually this **is** present, good catch reversed: Scene 6 does hit it. Scene 9 also hits it correctly ("That badge you picked up Monday..."). No defect here after re-check — retracting this sub-point.
- The notebook motif, however, has a real gap: the bible says Scene 1 seeds it, and "Scenes 7-8" are where it becomes "the literal mechanism ... documentation decisions." Scene 7 uses it explicitly ("Alex opens the field notebook and writes down exactly what happened"). **Scene 8 never mentions the notebook at all** — the Document node ("Alex's Chart") describes Alex writing observations but calls it "the same field notebook habit from day one" only in the *node-unlock narration*, which is good — but the Field Notes entry for that same node ("Alex's Chart — Writing What Was Seen") never uses the word "notebook," calling it "the clinical record" instead, and the visual concept section calls the hotspot "the papers/chart Alex holds," not the notebook. This is an inconsistent object: Scene 1 seeds a **paper field notebook** as a specific physical prop on the desk; Scene 8's visual concept re-invents it as "Alex's chart/paper in her own hands" tied to a *different* piece of art (`scene-08-neglect-abuse/v1.png`) where Alex is depicted "with chart in hand" — likely a tablet or clipboard, not the notebook object. The continuity device (a specific physical notebook, carried forward) risks becoming genericized into "whatever documentation prop is in the stock art," which is exactly the kind of drift the bible's continuity-devices section was written to prevent.

**1d. Marcus Odom (recurring peer) — CONFIRMED MISSING**
The character sheet establishes **Marcus Odom, PT** as a recurring peer who "occasionally texts/compares notes with Alex." He does not appear anywhere in any of the 9 scene storyboards. This may be intentional light-touch texture cut for scope, but as written the bible promises a recurring peer thread that never materializes even once across the entire week — a planted Chekhov's gun that never fires. Worth flagging as either (a) an intentional cut that should be reflected back into the bible, or (b) a missed beat, since Scene 4's "Team Discrepancy" node (PT documentation mismatch) would have been a natural, low-cost place to put Marcus's name on the PT note instead of an anonymous PT.

## 2. Timeline Breaks

**2a. Scene 6 vs. Scene 7/8 day-of-week slippage — CONFIRMED**
- Scene 5 ends "Dana will walk Alex through exactly what the agency expects... before Alex starts taking on visits without a preceptor" and is dated **Wednesday morning**.
- Scene 6 is dated **"Wednesday afternoon"** and ends "Thursday, Alex will be in Mr. Torres's living room."
- Scene 7 opens **"It's Thursday"** / "It's Thursday afternoon" — consistent with Scene 6's forward pointer. Good.
- Scene 8 opens **"It's Thursday afternoon, the same week Alex has been shadowing"** and is explicitly the same day as Scene 7 (its Story Beat says "later that afternoon," continuing directly from the Torres visit). But the Week Structure table in the bible maps Scene 8 to **"Thu/Fri"**, and Scene 7's own scene-complete narration says "in a few minutes, Alex is going to notice something on Mr. Torres's arm" — meaning Scene 8's Branch 2 bruising moment is supposed to be a *continuation of the same Torres visit*, minutes later, not "later that afternoon at a second home" as Scene 8's Story Beat actually stages it ("at an older patient's home... Alex notices new bruising"). Scene 7 promises an almost-immediate continuation; Scene 8 delivers a separate, unnamed, later home instead. This is a direct contradiction between the two scenes' own texts, not just an ambiguity — Scene 7's bridge line is simply false against what Scene 8 actually depicts.

**2b. Scene 9's implied timeline references content inconsistent with what Scenes 6–8 actually established**
Scene 9's Vision Pillars Recap Field Note says "Regulatory Leadership" as a pillar name and cites it as one of the four pillars Alex learned — but Scene 3 explicitly renamed the four pillars to **Clinical Excellence, Workforce Growth, Continuous Improvement, Community Trust**, and Scene 3's own QA Risks section flags "Regulatory Leadership" as a **retired/incorrect** pillar name from an older draft that must not ship. Scene 9's Vision Pillars Recap node ("Regulatory Leadership = survey-ready every day") and its Narration Plan ("Four pillars... Clinical Excellence, Workforce Development, Regulatory Leadership, Community Trust") reintroduce the exact retired names (**"Workforce Development"** and **"Regulatory Leadership"**) that Scene 3 spent an entire QA Risk explicitly correcting to Workforce Growth/Continuous Improvement. This is a real, confirmed regression: Scene 9 recaps a version of Scene 3 that no longer exists in this same document.

## 3. Tonal Violations

**3a. Melodrama / cartoonishness — none found at a severity worth flagging.** The drafts are consistently restrained; Scene 4 explicitly rejects the old SVG's "angry-son/cat animation" as cartoonish, which is the correct call per the tone rules.

**3b. Dry-slide/instructional-lecture regression in Scene 9 — CONFIRMED, mild**
Scene 9's Field Notes largely read as competent in-character recall, but two entries slip into disembodied textbook voice rather than Alex's or Dana's voice: "Home Health Difference Recap" ("Reference 42 CFR §484.110... sets the federal floor for what that documentation has to include") and "Survey Readiness & Remediation" ("The quiz ahead needs 80% to pass, with a 3-business-day retake window and a 3-attempt cap") are stated as flat policy facts, not attributed to Alex's spoken recall or Dana's voice the way the seam rules require ("instruction is always attributed to a character's knowledge or speech, never dropped in as disembodied narrator lecture"). This is a narration-plan/Field-Notes split issue: the Narration Plan versions of these same beats *are* in-character (Alex says the numbers to Dana), but the separate Field Notes text reverts to unattributed exposition, which is a two-track inconsistency the seam rules were meant to prevent.

**3c. Feedback tone check — pass.** Scene 7 and 8's "incorrect" feedback for the reporting-adjacent distractor answers (e.g., "calling 911/APS would be correct under different circumstances") is handled with unusual care and explicitly flagged as a QA risk against over-generalization — this is a good design instinct, not a defect.

## 4. Missed Continuity Opportunities

**4a. Scene 7 doesn't gesture back to Scene 4's Okafor practice, despite the bible mandating it — CONFIRMED**
The bible states: "Scene 7/8 narration should explicitly gesture back ('This is the same instinct you practiced with Mr. Okafor — pause, observe, don't assume') — retrieval practice, not new content." Scene 7's narration never once mentions Okafor, Scene 4, or "the same instinct you practiced." Its only backward references are to Scene 5 (SNF-vs-home-health) and Scene 2/Dana's mission framing. This is a direct, specific, named requirement from the bible that Scene 7 fails to execute even though Scene 7 is precisely the scene the bible calls out by name.

**4b. Scene 8 also never references Okafor** — same gap, compounding 4a. Scene 8's Story Beat mentions "regulatory integrity... not just a phrase from orientation" but never ties the compassion/observation instinct back to the Okafor shadow visit specifically, despite Scene 8's Branch 2 being the *direct* mechanical descendant of Okafor's "Patient Dignity" primer node per Scene 4's own text ("Today's moment is Alex practicing... the full decision comes later, for real" — a promise Scene 4 makes that only Scene 6 partially redeems via the mandated-sentence callback, not Scenes 7/8's actual field application).

**4c. Scene 2's "refusal/refuse" exclusion vs. Scene 7's actual content — worth flagging as a near-miss, not a defect.** Scene 2's QA Risks explicitly bans the word "refuse/refusal" from Scene 2's Field Notes to reserve that ground for Scene 7. Scene 7 is in fact the first full refusal-handling teaching moment, which is correctly executed — this linkage works. No fix needed, noting as confirmed-good for completeness.

**4d. Scene 5's "remote escalation" node primes Scene 6's escalation chain but Scene 6 never calls back to it.** Scene 5 builds real vocabulary ("coordination is not the same skill as escalation... that's not a coordination task, it's an escalation") that is a near-verbatim preview of Scene 6's entire second learning node. Scene 6's narration opens cold with Dana's briefing and never says anything like "remember what you worked out yesterday about coordination versus escalation" — a missed one-sentence retrieval hook that would have been cheap and exactly in the spirit of the bible's "retrieval practice, not new content" instruction.
